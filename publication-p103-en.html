Analysis of Interaction Strategies of Patent Law Subjects Using an Evolutionary Game-Theoretic Approach

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М. Исследование стратегий взаимодействия субъектов патентного права с использованием эволюционного теоретико-игрового подхода // Инновации. 2025. № 2 (304). С. 99–108.

Full text

Research problem

The article studies how enterprises choose to protect and use intellectual property when they interact repeatedly. Their decisions depend on registration and enforcement costs, returns from the innovation, and the chance of defending their rights successfully. The study models these incentives in a setting in which strategies can change over time.

Approach and methods

The authors combine evolutionary game theory with simulation. Rights holders choose ways to protect their innovations, and followers assess whether to obtain a license or imitate the development. Utility calculations account for enforcement parameters, costs, and expected revenue. The paper specifies the assumptions and establishes a baseline scenario with moderate supervision.

Findings

A software implementation was used to test the baseline scenario. Licensing was part of the stable pattern of follower behavior under those assumptions. The experiment illustrates how repeated interactions can be used to study changes in strategy frequencies and utility values over the modeled period. The paper also outlines further experiments, including changes to the length of the innovation life cycle.

Practical relevance

The model gives researchers a common structure for comparing patent policy scenarios. Its findings need to be interpreted in light of the assumed payoffs and the specific enforcement conditions represented in the simulation.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords
