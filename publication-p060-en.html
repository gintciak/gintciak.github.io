Hierarchical Cybernetic Model of Oil Production Enterprise With Distributed Decision-Making Centers

Daria E. Fedyaevskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Saurav Dixit

Bibliographic citation

Hierarchical Cybernetic Model of Oil Production Enterprise With Distributed Decision-Making Centers // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems: Digital Transformation: What is the Company of Today?. Springer Nature Switzerland. 2023. Vol. 805. pp. 21–34.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

At an oil producer, some decisions are made centrally and others are guided by the objectives of subsidiaries and field-level units. Conflicts between local objectives make it difficult to select actions for the enterprise as a whole. The chapter formalizes this problem for a hierarchy with distributed decision-making centers.

Approach and methods

The study combines a cybernetic representation of control actions and feedback with game theory and multi-agent modeling. For the strategic, tactical and operational levels, it identifies the responsible organizations and roles, their objectives, and the information exchanged during decision-making.

Findings

The result is a conceptual model of a three-level system. Agents represent the parent organization, a subsidiary and field management. The model gives an explicit account of local objectives and dependencies relevant to coordination. Control actions are represented alongside feedback about the state of the system being controlled. The specification of detailed strategies and the development of algorithms for solving the games are identified as subsequent work.

Practical relevance

The framework can guide the design of decision support for oil production. It helps establish which decisions are taken at each level and which information needs to be passed between levels.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Multi-agent systems
  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Intelligent decision support systems
