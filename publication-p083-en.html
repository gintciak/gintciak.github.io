Cybernetic Formalization of Management Tasks with Bounded Rationality in Organizational Systems

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М. Кибернетическая формализация задач управления с учетом ограниченной рациональности в организационных системах // Материалы XII Международной научно-практической конференции "Интеллектуальная инженерная экономика и индустрия 6.0" (ИНПРОМ-2025). 2025. С. 492–495.

Library record

Research problem

Several decision makers may influence the same organizational system and interpret its state differently. The paper examines how bounded rationality changes the standard cybernetic control problem, in which an action is chosen using feedback and assessed against an objective function.

Approach and methods

The authors add transformation functions to the feedback loop to represent bounded rationality in the formation of a control action and the interpretation of feedback. The formulation is extended to multiple decision makers who may coordinate or act independently. The controlled object's state is represented by its attributes, and an action is described as a transformation from a perceived state towards a target state.

Findings

The paper formalizes a generic control task with distributed decision centres. It shows how moving from the standard loop to this setting increases the number of variables and transformations that must be specified. Further interacting controlled objects add to that modelling burden.

Practical relevance

The formulation can guide model design by clarifying whose perception of the state influences a decision and how the actions of different decision makers are related. Applying the framework to specific management problems is identified as subsequent work.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Multi-agent systems
  • Game theory and strategic interaction

Keywords

bounded rationality, cybernetic model, distributed control, feedback, organizational systems
