A Cybernetic Approach to Modeling the Life Cycle of a Self-Organizing Work Group with Individual Goals and Capabilities

Шарко П.А., Гинцяк А.М.

eLIBRARY recordProceedings contents

Research problem

The paper addresses the life cycle of a group whose members organize their work jointly and differ in their goals and capabilities.

Approach and methods

The authors use a cybernetic perspective to frame the group life cycle while accounting for individual members' characteristics.

Practical relevance

The subject is relevant to modeling team activity in decentralized organizational systems.

Research areas

  • Multi-agent systems
  • Project and portfolio management
