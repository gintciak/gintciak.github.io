Heuristic Approach to Planning Complex Multi-Stage Production Systems

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Irina V. Vatamaniuk, Karina A. Lundaeva, Aleksei M. Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Irina V. Vatamaniuk, Karina A. Lundaeva, Aleksei M. Gintciak. Heuristic Approach to Planning Complex Multi-Stage Production Systems // International Journal of Technology. 2023. Vol. 14. No. 8. pp. 1790–1799.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

In multi-stage production, items pass through a fixed sequence of operations that use different resources. Sites have limited capacities, and the available production data can vary in completeness and reliability. The article investigates how to construct a satisfactory plan under these conditions and within a practical computation budget.

Approach and methods

The problem formulation describes a network of enterprises producing homogeneous goods through a strictly ordered sequence of operations. The authors propose a greedy heuristic that includes elements of local optimization and ranks candidate assignments using a penalty function. It has separate branches for cases in which the production bottleneck is known in advance and cases in which it must be identified by the procedure.

Findings

The algorithm was tested on a heavy industry planning task with a horizon of five to ten years. It produces a quasi-optimal plan. The authors identify the distance to the global optimum as an open question and note that computation time increases with the number of products, production units and operations.

Practical relevance

The method targets sectors with long production cycles and relatively few items, such as shipbuilding and aircraft manufacturing. The resulting plans can serve as candidates for capacity analysis and further adjustment.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

multi-stage manufacturing, production planning, greedy algorithm, bottleneck, quasi-optimization
