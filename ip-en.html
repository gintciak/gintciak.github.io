Research

Intellectual property management

Modelling patent strategies, managing portfolios and analysing relationships across assets.

With collaborators, I study how intellectual property protection and patent rules are linked to decisions by firms. Game theory offers a way to represent how costs and benefits are distributed across the parties involved.

A related stream focuses on managing intellectual property portfolios. We examine how semantic similarity among assets can be represented and how internal relationships can inform portfolio composition.
