Analysis of Environmental Monitoring Systems Near Large Transportation Systems

Alexandra Petryaeva, Marina Bolsunovskaya, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksandr Leksashov, Andrey Zhukov, Aleksander Kuptsov, Aleksei Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

Alexandra Petryaeva, Marina Bolsunovskaya, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksandr Leksashov, Andrey Zhukov, Aleksander Kuptsov, Aleksei Gintciak. Analysis of Environmental Monitoring Systems Near Large Transportation Systems // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems. Springer International Publishing. 2023. No. 1 (509). pp. 1385–1393.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Water bodies located near major roads and other infrastructure require regular observation. This chapter examines how monitoring tools can bring different kinds of information about a water body together and make it available for environmental decisions.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed existing approaches to environmental water monitoring and surveyed patents for monitoring technologies. The review informed requirements for a system within the Digital Uskat project. The chapter describes the data entering the system, how those data relate to sources of environmental impact and which functions are needed to process them.

Findings

The study presents a functional structure and explains how the proposed system would operate. It combines measurements of water conditions with information from other sources. Planned functions include recording conditions throughout the day, accumulating statistics for timely decisions and generating reports.

Practical relevance

The design addresses sites where major transport or other infrastructure is close to rivers and reservoirs. It helps specify which observations are needed for a timely response and how they can be collected. The chapter presents a system design and identifies the benefits expected from its use.
