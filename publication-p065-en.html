Innovation Management in the Context of Digital Transformation

Executive editor · А. М. Гинцяк

Bibliographic citation

DOILibrary record

Research problem

This volume brings together papers from the third national student research conference on innovation management in the context of digital transformation, held at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University on 12 and 13 April 2024. The conference examined how digital tools and mathematical models can inform decisions in organizations, industry and innovation management.

Approach and methods

The volume has six sections on innovation, mathematical decision models, control in organizational systems, project management, product management and quality management. The contributions cover systems analysis, business analysis, risk assessment and management methods in individual industries. Examples include mathematical models of airlines, intellectual property in collaborative projects and the prediction of fuel consumption.

Findings

The proceedings publish contributions by students, doctoral students, early-career researchers and staff of educational and research organizations. I served as the volume's executive editor. It was published by POLYTECH-PRESS in St. Petersburg with ISBN 978-5-7422-8664-6.

Practical relevance

The volume gives students and supervisors examples of how to formulate research projects and communicate their results. Individual papers can serve as seminar readings in innovation management, quality management and mathematical modelling.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intellectual property management
