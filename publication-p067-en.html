Algorithm for Estimating the Convergence of Stochastic Pareto Optimization

Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М., Дергачев М.В.

Bibliographic citation

Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М., Дергачев М.В. Алгоритм оценки сходимости стохастической Парето-оптимизации // Информационные технологии и вычислительные системы. 2024. № 4. С. 91–99.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

The paper asks when a stochastic search has explored enough scenarios to estimate a Pareto front with useful accuracy. In multi-objective optimization, progress concerns a set of trade-offs. A stopping criterion therefore needs to track changes in the approximated front while accounting for the computation required to evaluate additional scenarios.

Approach and methods

The algorithm generates random scenarios at increasing sample sizes. For each size it constructs a front, selects a strictly convex approximation that the authors call the strong front, and computes the area below it. Early estimates are averaged over repeated Monte Carlo runs. The search checks the relative change in area between successive sample sizes against a specified threshold.

Findings

The authors implement the algorithm in Java and plot the relationship between the number of scenarios, the front area and the accuracy setting. These plots illustrate how the procedure detects a slowdown in improvement of the chosen measure. The paper discusses sensitivity to the quality of generated scenarios and the extra work involved in repeating runs at small sample sizes.

Practical relevance

The measure can help manage the computational budget when scoring a scenario requires evaluating a model. Its threshold and the interpretation of a stop depend on the variability of the evaluations and on how well the measure represents the trade-offs of interest.
