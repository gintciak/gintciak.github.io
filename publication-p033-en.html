Conference paper · 2022

Building and Developing an Early-Career Research Team in Digital Modeling

Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А. Формирование и развитие молодёжного научного коллектива в области цифрового моделирования // Санкт-Петербургский конгресс «Профессиональное образование, наука и инновации в XXI веке»: сборник докладов. 2022. С. 51–55.

Research problem

An early-career research lab must deliver ongoing projects while training staff who are new to research. The paper identifies three factors that shape how such a lab can operate. Researchers develop their ability to produce results over time, students need to work with experienced colleagues, and links to academic and industry partners take time to establish.

Approach and methods

The case study is the Digital Modeling of Industrial Systems Laboratory at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. A matrix links projects to three working groups whose responsibilities cover analytical and simulation models, software implementation, and data analysis.

Findings

Each intersection in the matrix identifies which members of a working group contribute to a project. Experienced researchers mentor newer colleagues, and assignments across several projects expose junior staff to different tasks before they specialize. The paper presents this structure as an organizational case study. It also argues that early assessments of a new lab need to account for the development of its staff’s research skills.

Practical relevance

The matrix gives a lab head a practical basis for assigning researchers across projects and pairing early-career staff with mentors. Labs in other fields can use the same arrangement to document responsibilities and plan staff development.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Digital modelling of production systems

Keywords

early-career researchers, matrix structure, mentoring, digital modeling, research teams
