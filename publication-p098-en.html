Comparison of Tools and Methods of Forming an IT Project Team in the Context of Cascading and Flexible Project Development

Дергачев М.В., Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Дергачев М.В., Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М. Сравнение инструментов и методов формирования команды ИТ-проекта в контексте разработки каскадных и гибких проектов // Sustainable development and engineering economics. 2025. Т. 2. С. 7–24.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

The paper examines how specialists are selected for software development projects. The importance of skills, compatibility between colleagues, and flexibility to adjust the team depends on how the work is organized. The authors compare team formation approaches for waterfall and agile projects, taking account of differences between these management practices.

Approach and methods

The study combines a critical literature review with a comparison of models and instruments. It identifies five groups of models, including fuzzy cognitive and regression models, systems analysis, grade and experience-based assessment, and competency trees. The instruments discussed include expert assessment, game theory, systems analysis, single and multiple objective optimization, and simulation.

Findings

Single-objective formulations were common in the reviewed material. Coverage of multiple objectives for waterfall projects was limited. The authors identify aspects of communication and team dynamics that existing approaches represent only partially. They propose multi-objective team selection for waterfall projects as an area for further development.

Practical relevance

Project managers and staffing specialists can use the review to identify relevant selection criteria and methods for evaluating candidates. For researchers, it highlights where models could usefully represent changes in team composition and interactions among members.
