Software Package for Cloud Storage Development and Visualization of a Laser Scanning Point Cloud for Natural and Technical Systems

Aleksei Gintciak, Svetlana Shirokova, Marina Bolsunovskaya, Alexander Leksashow, Aleksandra Loginova

Bibliographic citation

Aleksei Gintciak, Svetlana Shirokova, Marina Bolsunovskaya, Alexander Leksashow, Aleksandra Loginova. Software Package for Cloud Storage Development and Visualization of a Laser Scanning Point Cloud for Natural and Technical Systems // 35th International Business Information Management Association Conference (IBIMA). International Business Information Management Association. Seville, Spain, 1–2 April 2020. 2020. ISBN 978-0-9998551-4-0.

Publication page

Research problem

Laser scanning of heritage sites and road infrastructure produces point clouds alongside photographs. This paper considers how to process, store and visualise those materials within one software package.

Approach and methods

The authors review approaches to working with these data and examine materials from field acquisition. The publisher's abstract identifies aerial, mobile and terrestrial scanning. It describes the development of a set of processing algorithms and the design of a system architecture and functional scheme.

Findings

The software modules were integrated into an experimental package for processing, storing and viewing point clouds. The publisher's abstract reports that the authors tested and refined the prototype.

Practical relevance

The sites and scanning methods provide the context in which the authors identify requirements for handling the collected materials. The design can inform workflows that need to manage point clouds and photographs together.

Research areas

  • Transport modelling and optimization

Keywords
