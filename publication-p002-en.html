Selecting a Project and Staff Workload Management System for an Innovative Company

Широкова С.В., Болсуновская М.В., Гинцяк А.М., Жарова М.С.

Труды Третьей международной научно-практической конференции. 2018. С. 747–755.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

The study considers software that can handle project records and give managers a clear picture of employee workload. The practical question is how to reconcile staffing and schedules for ongoing assignments with the possibility of launching new projects.

Approach and methods

The authors compile a feature list for six systems. It covers time accounting, access control, messaging, Gantt charts, reporting, issue tracking, and availability on web and mobile platforms. A quantitative procedure based on functional completeness uses matrices of similarities and differences between sets of features. Price is considered when the final choice is made.

Findings

The paper recommends Easy Redmine with an additional personnel management module for the company studied. It explains how prices for cloud and on-premises options can change the order of preference. Issue tracking was a particularly important requirement for this user.

Practical relevance

The comparison can support the preparation of requirements for a corporate project management system. The proposed module supports a concrete management workflow that starts with a visual review of staff capacity and continues with rescheduling tasks and reassigning work where overload is identified.
