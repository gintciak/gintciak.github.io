The Influence of Communication Strategies of Intelligent Agents in Production Systems on the Shift of Sustainable Solutions

Polina A. Sharko, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Salbek M. Beketov, Karina A. Lundaeva

Bibliographic citation

Polina A. Sharko, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Salbek M. Beketov, Karina A. Lundaeva. The Influence of Communication Strategies of Intelligent Agents in Production Systems on the Shift of Sustainable Solutions // Sustainability. 2025. Vol. 17. No. 24. Article 11130.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

How does the stability of a production plan change when operating units have different preferences for cooperation? In decentralized production systems, overall objectives have to be reconciled with local performance targets. This study examines how constraints on an agent's targets influence communication choices and the resulting performance of the system.

Approach and methods

The authors use a multi-agent model for scheduling oilfield operations. A coefficient represents the extent to which an agent accounts for other participants' interests in its objective function. The authors run computational experiments at different coefficient values and use Kruskal–Wallis and Dunn's tests to compare outcomes. They consider robust, evolutionary, and strategic stability.

Findings

The paper proposes a procedure for identifying constraints that account for both local objectives and the system's aggregate performance target. For the tested data, revising the individual field plans produced an additional increase of about 1% in predicted oil production relative to a scenario with optimized crew allocation and well intervention scheduling. This estimate belongs to the specific simulation case.

Practical relevance

The procedure can help production planners evaluate how local performance constraints influence cooperation. A model transferred to another region would require new estimates of its parameters and local targets.
