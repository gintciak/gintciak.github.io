Digital Platform for Modeling the Development of Regional Innovation Systems of Russian Federation

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Tatiana Yu. Kudryavtseva, Irina A. Rudskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Denis O. Zhidkov, Darya E. Fedyaevskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya

Bibliographic citation

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Tatiana Yu. Kudryavtseva, Irina A. Rudskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Denis O. Zhidkov, Darya E. Fedyaevskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya. Digital Platform for Modeling the Development of Regional Innovation Systems of Russian Federation // International Journal of Technology. 2023. Vol. 14. No. 8. pp. 1779–1789.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

The research develops a digital tool for examining how scientific and technological development relates to regional socioeconomic conditions. The platform is designed to work with data on the innovation infrastructure and industrial composition of Russian regions, calculate indicators and present them to users.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed platforms for the analysis of socioeconomic systems, specified requirements and selected implementation tools. TypeScript was used on the client and server. The study describes data workflows for different user roles, including automated acquisition and processing and the presentation of information in visual form.

Findings

The article presents a software prototype with user accounts, a simulation experiment module and tools for data analysis and visualization. It describes the role of each component and the technical decisions used to integrate them. This architecture provides a basis for further development of forecasting and scenario tools.

Practical relevance

The prototype is intended for researchers, companies, investors and other users of regional data. Its documented requirements and design can inform platforms in other socioeconomic domains, with data sources and calculation procedures specified for the new application.

Research areas

  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords

regional innovation systems, digital platform, sustainable development, data processing, visualization
