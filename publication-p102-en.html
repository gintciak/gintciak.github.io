Bounded Rationality of Agents in the Management of Innovative Projects

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М. Ограниченная рациональность агентов в задачах управления инновационными проектами // Инновации. 2025. № 2 (304). С. 62–70.

Full text

Research problem

Innovation projects involve several decision makers, each with partial information and limited capacity to process it. The authors investigate how these constraints could enter predictions of management outcomes. They focus on the gap between the state of the managed system and how its participants perceive that state.

Approach and methods

An innovation project is represented as an organizational system with distributed centers of decision making. The paper proposes a hypergraph representation for the structure of interactions and a state space for the managed object. A function representing bounded rationality connects the underlying state with an individual's perception and capacity to communicate with other agents. The approach includes causal relationships that may be hidden from some participants.

Findings

The result is a method for introducing bounded rationality into predictions for innovation project management. It provides a way to express subjective assessments and to examine how they might affect the range of outcomes after a decision. The authors describe the basis of the method as heuristic and identify validation on actual organizational systems as further work.

Practical relevance

The method can help formulate requirements for decision support, particularly when a model needs to specify what participants know, what they communicate, and how they interpret information.

Research areas

  • Multi-agent systems
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

bounded rationality, innovation projects, hypergraph, state space, management decisions
