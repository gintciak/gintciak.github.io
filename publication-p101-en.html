Multi-Agent Systems in Modeling the Development of Self-Organizing Work Groups. Part 1. Development of a System of Criteria

Шарко П.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Шарко П.А., Гинцяк А.М. Мультиагентные системы при моделировании развития самоорганизующихся рабочих групп. Часть 1. Разработка системы критериев // Инновации. 2025. № 2 (304). С. 38–46.

Full text

Research problem

The paper addresses work groups in which decision authority is distributed among members. Group composition, technology choices, and resource use depend on individual goals, knowledge, and capabilities. The authors ask what a modeling tool must be able to represent if it is to help study how such groups develop.

Approach and methods

The study starts by describing organizational structure and the work carried out by a group. It considers interactions among individual workers, organizational units, and the wider organization. From this domain description, the authors derive modeling requirements, including support for several levels of management, participant interdependence, and differences in how outcomes are assessed. Self-organization is considered under external constraints on the group's resources.

Findings

The first part of the research delivers a set of criteria for assessing modeling tools and a domain-based approach to describing groups. The criteria cover different control networks, relationships between individual and group tasks, and the ways participants can influence group development.

Practical relevance

The criteria can be used to compare candidate approaches when preparing a model of a decentralized organization. Where a tool satisfies only some of the requirements, the authors propose combining it with complementary tools in a hybrid model.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Project and portfolio management

Keywords
