Classification of Methods of One-Dimensional Stochastic Optimization

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М.

Bibliographic citation

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М. Классификация методов одномерной стохастической оптимизации // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 303–307.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

Several stochastic algorithms can search for the global optimum of a nonlinear or multimodal function of one variable. The paper organizes these methods so that readers can compare search mechanisms in terms of the requirements of a particular problem.

Approach and methods

The review covers basic random sampling, adaptive random search, simulated annealing, basin hopping and the cross-entropy method. Algorithms are compared by how the search is directed, how information from earlier iterations is used and the complexity of parameter tuning.

Findings

The classification distinguishes random sampling, stochastic local and global search with probabilistic moves, and methods that adapt a sampling distribution. The use of previous results ranges from independent trials to local adaptation, search history and updates based on sample statistics. The framework makes clear where a given algorithm uses information gathered during the search.

Practical relevance

The classification can guide the initial selection of algorithm families and the design of a comparison experiment. It helps specify the desired search behavior, tuning requirements and use of objective evaluations already collected.

Research areas

  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

Keywords

one-dimensional optimization, stochastic algorithms, random search, simulated annealing, cross-entropy
