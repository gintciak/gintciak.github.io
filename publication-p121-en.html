An Information-Based Approach to Assessing Synergy among Innovation Project Risks

Дыбулин В.М., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Дыбулин В.М., Гинцяк А.М. Применение информационного подхода для оценки синергетического эффекта рисков инновационного проекта // Интеллектуальная инженерная экономика и Индустрия X.0 (ИНПРОМ-2026). Сборник трудов Международной научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 271–275.

eLIBRARY recordProceedings contents

Research problem

The paper addresses the combined effect of risks in an innovation project, focusing on links among risks and their potential consequences for the project.

Approach and methods

It considers an information-based approach to assessing this effect.

Practical relevance

The subject is relevant to the assessment of risk combinations when making decisions about an innovation project.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems

innovation project, project risks, synergy, information-based assessment
