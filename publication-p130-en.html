Method for Dynamic Analysis of a Semantic Graph of an Intellectual Property Portfolio

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М. Метод динамического анализа семантического графа портфеля объектов интеллектуальной собственности // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 191–197.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

An intellectual property portfolio changes when new developments become available and technology priorities shift. The paper asks how to track the relationships among research and development outputs and changes in technology clusters over time.

Approach and methods

The assets are represented by nodes in a technology graph. Each asset has vector representations of its application area, technical problem and technologies. Pairwise semantic similarities are converted to edge weights that represent synergy, potential reuse or substitutability. New assets and revised weights update the graph. An inertia parameter connects successive versions.

Findings

The study specifies rules for calculating interaction coefficients and a mechanism for updating the graph over time. This representation can be used to compare successive portfolio snapshots and study changes in technology clusters and the relations among assets.

Practical relevance

The method can inform reviews of a research-intensive enterprise's intellectual property portfolio. It provides a basis for flagging overlaps, spotting opportunities to reuse technologies and asking domain experts to investigate changes in the portfolio.

Research areas
