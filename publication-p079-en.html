Digitalization Challenges in Third-Party Logistics

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М. Проблемы в области 3PL-логистики в условиях цифровизации // Автоматизация в промышленности. 2025. № 3. С. 50–54.

Journal websiteeLIBRARY record

Research problem

Third-party logistics providers serve clients with different cargo requirements, accounting rules and information systems. The paper examines how these differences shape warehouse digitalization when a provider has to modernize its own infrastructure while accommodating customer processes.

Approach and methods

The analysis draws on a regional warehouse provider and publicly available sources. The authors organize causes and effects in a table covering database integration, equipment obsolescence, warehouse management functionality, dependence on customers, employee training and analytics for forecasting workloads and building performance measures.

Findings

The paper identifies six groups of problems and explains which are especially visible when many clients share a provider. Differences in data and operating requirements complicate standardization, staff training and performance comparison. The authors discuss simplifying handheld interfaces, integrating warehouse and enterprise systems, and assessing the prospects of proposed arrangements with business partners.

Practical relevance

The cause-and-effect analysis can help connect software requirements with warehouse operations, staff training and collaboration with customers. It can also inform discussion of the operating conditions for a proposed automation project.

Research areas
