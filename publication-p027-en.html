Research problem

The volume brings together papers based on presentations at the All-Russian Student Educational and Scientific Conference held on 22–23 April 2022 at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. Aleksei Gintciak is listed on the editorial board in the Russian imprint.

Approach and methods

The proceedings cover innovation management, applied systems analysis, project management and quality management. The contributions range from problem definition and the choice of mathematical methods to applications of digital tools in particular organisations. The collection includes work on requirements for information systems, modelling of technological processes and optimisation of production plans.

Practical relevance

The proceedings document examples of student research on the management of organisational systems. The individual papers can support seminar discussion, provide examples of how applied problems are framed, and illustrate choices of method for related studies.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
