The Integration of Information Systems in a Medical Facility to Improve the Efficiency of Decision-Making

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Alexander Leksashov, Aleksey Gintciak, Anastasiia Shirokova

Bibliographic citation

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Alexander Leksashov, Aleksey Gintciak, Anastasiia Shirokova. The Integration of Information Systems in a Medical Facility to Improve the Efficiency of Decision-Making // E3S Web of Conferences. EDP Sciences. 2020. Vol. 224. Article 03029.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Medical records and diagnostic examinations can be managed in different information systems. Manually copying a new order or a change to patient details between these systems can introduce delays and data-entry errors. The article examines an integration project designed to coordinate these processes.

Approach and methods

The authors connect a medical information system and a diagnostic information system through Mirth Connect, an independent integration platform. Message receipt, transformation and routing are configured in the platform. The design covers transmission of new examination orders, preparation of equipment worklists and synchronisation of updates to identification data.

Findings

In the implemented workflow, new orders were sent to the diagnostic system. Changes to patient data in the medical system were automatically reflected in the linked system. The integration platform handled the rules for those exchanges while preserving the existing code and configuration of the medical and diagnostic systems.

Practical relevance

This arrangement supports the organisation of examinations and reduces repeated data entry. The case identifies the information that needs to stay aligned when building diagnostic worklists from clinical orders.
