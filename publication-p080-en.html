Complex Improvement of Research Work of Master's Students in the Field of Innovation and Quality Management

Редько С.Г., Гинцяк А.М., Селедцова И.А., Бурлуцкая Ж.В.

Bibliographic citation

Редько С.Г., Гинцяк А.М., Селедцова И.А., Бурлуцкая Ж.В. Комплексное совершенствование научно-исследовательской работы студентов магистерских программ в области инноватики и управления качеством // Информатизация образования и науки. 2025. № 2 (66). С. 114–127.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

The paper examines how master's student research is organized. It identifies difficulties in choosing a topic and supervisor, unclear expectations for interim results and limited meeting time for substantive research discussion. The setting is the Innovation Studies and Quality Management programmes at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University.

Approach and methods

The pilot project brings together organizational changes, teaching material and a digital platform. It covers matching students with supervisors by topic, planning work in stages, reviewing interim outputs and regular discussion in research groups. The platform is designed to provide short instructional materials, templates and planning tools.

Findings

The authors describe the design of the educational product and the conditions for adapting it to different programmes. A modular structure allows the teaching materials and platform functions to be updated independently. The paper discusses thesis quality, publishing activity and student engagement as intended outcomes and describes the mechanisms the pilot is designed to test.

Practical relevance

Programme coordinators can draw on the design when setting research milestones and assessment criteria or producing materials for independent study. A shared platform can cover common requirements, leaving meetings with supervisors for the substance of individual research projects.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems
