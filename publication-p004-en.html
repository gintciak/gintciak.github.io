IT Project Team Management Based on a Network-Centric Model

M. V. Bolsunovskaya, S. V. Shirokova, A. V. Loginova, A. M. Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

M. V. Bolsunovskaya, S. V. Shirokova, A. V. Loginova, A. M. Gintciak. IT Project Team Management Based on a Network-Centric Model // Proceedings of 2018 17th Russian Scientific and Practical Conference on Planning and Teaching Engineering Staff for the Industrial and Economic Complex of the Region, PTES 2018. IEEE. 2018. pp. 165–168.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

This study examines interactions among specialists working in a distributed IT project. In a team where developers, engineers and researchers work in parallel, a formal reporting chart captures only part of the organisation. Evaluating coordination also requires examining the links through which people exchange information and work together.

Approach and methods

The authors develop a network-centric representation of a project team. They propose combining graph metrics, sociometric methods and information measures of integrity and autonomy. Their example is a team developing hardware and software to predict failures in data storage systems. The paper reports network indicators and compares two organisational structures for the group.

Findings

The team network had 24 nodes, about 120 links and a reported density of 0.43. Nodes with high degree centrality were associated with project coordination. In the calculated alternative, which assigns the project manager fewer assistants, the autonomy measure increased from 0.45 to 0.56.

Practical relevance

The method offers a way to compare distributions of managerial responsibility. It can help examine the formation of subgroups and identify participants who handle a substantial share of project communications.
