Teaching

I teach mathematical and computer modelling, game theory and decision methods at SPbPU. In class we start with an applied problem, formulate a model and discuss how to test whether it can support the decision at hand.

Master’s programme and open courses

  • Mathematical and computer modelling for high-tech production management

    SPbPU master’s programme · 27.04.05_13 · programme director since 2026

    The programme combines production management with applied mathematics. Graduates earn qualifications in both Innovation Studies (27.04.05) and Applied Mathematics and Computer Science (01.04.02). I helped develop the programme and teach courses on modelling organisational systems and systems analysis.

  • Game Theory in Innovation Management

    Practical course · master’s level · since academic year 2020/21

    Students work through cooperative and non-cooperative games, formalise participants’ interests and test decisions in interactive exercises. The course is a module of Mathematical Models of Technical Control Systems. I wrote a companion textbook for independent study.
