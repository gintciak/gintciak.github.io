Agent-Based Modeling of Tourist Flow Distribution Based on the Analysis of Tourist Preferences

Kirillov Dmitriy, Zhanna Burlutskaya, Aleksei Gintciak, Daria Zubkova

Bibliographic citation

Kirillov Dmitriy, Zhanna Burlutskaya, Aleksei Gintciak, Daria Zubkova. Agent-Based Modeling of Tourist Flow Distribution Based on the Analysis of Tourist Preferences // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems. Springer Nature Switzerland. 2023. Vol. 684. pp. 360–369.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Visitors can evaluate the same destinations quite differently, which makes the treatment of preferences important in tourism demand models. The chapter asks how to specify destination choice for several types of tourists while allowing destination attributes themselves to change.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed agent-based studies of tourism and proposed a structure in which both visitors and destinations are agents. The model features three broad tourist groups and five destinations resembling Russian resorts. It reflects differences in the importance of destination features for groups with different demographic characteristics, including age and income.

Findings

The chapter formulates a choice rule based on a weighted evaluation of the destination attributes, with provisions for inertia in travel choices and for changes in destination characteristics. It considers mobile network operator data as a potential source for model development. Software implementation and testing on empirical data are identified as subsequent work.

Practical relevance

The structure can guide the design of experiments on how changes in services or resort characteristics might redistribute demand. Such experiments can complement analyses of which tourism offers appeal to particular segments.

Research areas

  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords

agent-based modeling, tourism demand, tourist flows, tourist preferences, destination choice
