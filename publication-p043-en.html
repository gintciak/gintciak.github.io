Use and Processing of Digital Data in the Era of Industry 4.0

Aleksei Gintciak, Zhanna Burlutskaya, Darya Fedyaevskaya, Artem Budkin

Bibliographic citation

Aleksei Gintciak, Zhanna Burlutskaya, Darya Fedyaevskaya, Artem Budkin. Use and Processing of Digital Data in the Era of Industry 4.0 // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems. Springer Nature Switzerland. 2023. Vol. 684. pp. 468–480.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Data produced by digital services offer new ways to study socioeconomic processes. Using them in a model requires matching the source to the task and establishing suitable conditions for acquisition, processing and storage. This chapter organizes experience with several classes of digital data.

Approach and methods

The review draws on 25 publications from Russian and international sources. It compares data from mobile network operators, social networks, applications and websites. The authors distinguish sources from collection technologies and survey uses in statistics, marketing, traffic analysis, assessment of network coverage and models of user behavior.

Findings

The study presents a classification of data sources, acquisition methods and applications. It identifies issues for data protection at multiple points in the process and considers a methodological framework from the European Statistical System for working with new sources of statistical data. It also discusses problems that arise when processing unstructured data streams.

Practical relevance

The review helps teams choose inputs for a digital model. For each potential source, they can consider which indicators it supports, how the data will be processed and stored, and which safeguards need to form part of the system design.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Modelling socioeconomic systems

Keywords

digital data, Industry 4.0, data collection, data protection, socioeconomic modeling
