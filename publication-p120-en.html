Research problem

This conference paper addresses the revision of probabilistic task duration estimates as new information about a project becomes available.

Approach and methods

The proposed approach uses Bayes' theorem to update the parameters of task duration distributions.

Practical relevance

The subject is relevant to maintaining current estimates in probabilistic project planning.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

Keywords

project planning, task duration, Bayes' theorem, parameter updating
