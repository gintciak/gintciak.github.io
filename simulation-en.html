Research

Simulation-based optimization

Using models to test interventions and find decisions that perform acceptably across scenarios.

I use simulation to estimate measures that are difficult to obtain analytically. A simulation reproduces the system’s dynamics under each candidate decision, while an optimization procedure searches the feasible alternatives under resource and time constraints.

I also study how to organize the experiment itself. Calibration, validation, repeated runs and sensitivity analysis help identify which recommendations depend on the initial assumptions. The publications cover both simulation optimization methods and the construction of models for these tasks.
