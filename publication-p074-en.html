Evolutionary Game Theory as a Tool for Analyzing Intellectual Property Management Strategies

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М. Эволюционная теория игр как инструмент анализа стратегий управления интеллектуальной собственностью // Управление качеством продукции на основе передовых производственных технологий: Сборник тезисов докладов VI Международного форума "Передовые цифровые и производственные технологии". 2024. С. 128–133.

Publication page

Research problem

The study examines the decisions of rights holders and companies interested in using an innovation. It asks how patent protection and the likelihood of detecting an infringement affect the choice between licensing and imitation, and how changing external conditions can be represented in a dynamic model.

Approach and methods

In a sequential game, the rights holder chooses a form of protection and the follower chooses whether to buy a licence or imitate the innovation. Payoffs include the return from using the innovation, the cost of protection or adoption and possible penalties. The authors solve the one-shot game by backward induction. For a dynamic extension, they propose updating probabilities as events are observed and studying alternative scenarios through simulation.

Findings

In the one-shot model, the follower chooses imitation when detection probabilities are low or moderate. The paper then outlines an evolutionary game model for analysing how strategies could change as new judicial precedents accumulate. Testing the stability of strategies under different scenarios is identified as subsequent work.

Practical relevance

The formulation can be used to set up scenarios for studying patent policy. It makes assumptions about enforcement, licence terms and legal protection explicit, including cases where rights holders use a smart contract to record information about an invention.

Research areas

  • Intellectual property management
  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords

evolutionary game theory, patent law, licensing strategy, smart contracts, simulation modelling
