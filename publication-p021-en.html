Innovative Project Model Development for the Use of Digital Wearable Devices to Improve the Quality of Life

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Nina Osipenko, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksei Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Nina Osipenko, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksei Gintciak. Innovative Project Model Development for the Use of Digital Wearable Devices to Improve the Quality of Life // Innovations in Digital Economy. SPBPU IDE 2021. Communications in Computer and Information Science. Springer International Publishing. 2022. Vol. 1619. pp. 133–142.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Wearable devices pass activity and physiological measurements to mobile applications. Designing such a system involves device compatibility, reliable data exchange and protection of the information collected. The chapter examines these requirements for consumer wearable devices.

Approach and methods

The authors compare the characteristics and market positions of major devices and use a fitness tracker to illustrate synchronisation. Their model follows data from the sensors to the mobile application and onward to storage. It considers Bluetooth Low Energy as an interface and identifies completeness and integrity of the transferred information as design considerations.

Findings

The chapter presents a synchronisation scheme and discusses requirements for reliable transmission. It identifies stages where the transmission from tracker to application and from application to storage needs particular attention. The authors recommend treating hardware and platform compatibility as part of the same design problem as information protection.

Practical relevance

The scheme can help specify software that uses wearable sensor data. It makes it possible to identify where completeness needs to be checked and to assign responsibilities to the device, the application and any external storage.

Research areas
