Mathematical Models of the Oil Production Process at Wells

Петряева А., Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Петряева А., Гинцяк А. Математические модели процесса нефтедобычи на скважинах // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: Материалы Всероссийской студенческой учебно-научной конференции. 2022. С. 130–135.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

Models of oil production support volume forecasting and the analysis of operational decisions. This review asks which tools are appropriate for different modelling objectives and how the choice depends on information about a well and the production process.

Approach and methods

The authors examine publications indexed in Scopus and identify three groups of methods. These are simulation, especially system dynamics, statistical time-series models and structural models. Examples include examining changes in observed production after external events and representing production rates in relation to a technological process and material balance.

Findings

The reviewed studies show where time-series models can be used to forecast production volumes. The choice between statistical and structural formulations depends in part on the available information about the technology. Simulation is useful when the analysis must explicitly represent components, flows and consequences of equipment failures.

Practical relevance

The review can guide the selection of methods for forecasting, design work and operational scenarios. It identifies the scale of the system, data availability and the relationships to be represented explicitly as considerations for an applied model.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

oil production, well modelling, time series, system dynamics, forecasting
