An Approach to Forming and Optimizing the Intellectual Property Portfolio of a Knowledge-Intensive Enterprise

Гинцяк А.М., Лундаева К.А.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М., Лундаева К.А. Подход к формированию и оптимизации портфеля объектов интеллектуальной собственности наукоемких производств // Цифровой инжиниринг и организация наукоемких производств: сборник тезисов докладов Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 74–84.

eLIBRARY recordProceedings record

Research problem

The value of an intellectual property portfolio depends on whether developments reinforce one another, can reuse shared technology or duplicate existing work. The paper presents a way to identify these interactions and select a coherent set of assets.

Approach and methods

Patent descriptions are converted to vectors and used to construct a weighted graph of semantic interactions. Positive weights denote potential synergy, and negative weights represent duplication or conflict. Signed spectral clustering identifies groups of related assets. A greedy search then removes the asset whose exclusion gives the largest improvement in the objective at each iteration.

Findings

The authors identified three technology clusters in a set of 35 assets from PATENTSCOPE. The greedy refinement improved the objective for two clusters as their sizes fell from eight to three and from twelve to nine assets, respectively. The third cluster was retained because no single removal improved the objective. The paper also reports measures of coherence and link density for the resulting groups.

Practical relevance

The method gives experts a traceable list of candidates to group together or remove. It can support the review of a research and development portfolio when assessing every pair of assets individually would be time consuming.
