Research

Game theory and strategic interaction

Strategy choice, incentives and interactions among stakeholders.

I apply game theory to decisions whose outcomes depend on the actions of other parties. Applications include forming research teams, designing incentives, competing for project funding and modelling interactions among patent stakeholders.

My publications cover sequential and simultaneous games, cooperative formulations and the evolution of strategies. I also study how ontologies can represent game formulations and support model selection.
