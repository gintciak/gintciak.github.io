Approaches to Modeling Strategic Interactions of Stakeholders to Support Decision-Making in Project Activities

Зубкова Д., Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Зубкова Д., Гинцяк А. Подходы к моделированию стратегических взаимодействий заинтересованных сторон для поддержки принятия решений в проектной деятельности // Цифровая трансформация экономических систем: проблемы и перспективы (ЭКОПРОМ–2022). Сборник трудов Всероссийской научно-практической конференции с зарубежным участием. 2022. С. 726–728.

Publication page

Research problem

Project stakeholders may pursue different objectives and influence the project through their decisions. The paper examines available methods for representing that influence and selecting tools for a model of successive strategic interactions.

Approach and methods

The authors search Scopus using combinations of terms for project stakeholders and classes of decision methods. They discuss formal and expert approaches, including simulation and game theory. The classes are considered in relation to their ability to represent actors, contextual conditions and the choice of strategies.

Findings

For the search queries used, expert methods and simulation were the most frequently identified approaches. The percentages reported in the paper are 54.9% and 16.5% respectively. The authors identify game theory as suitable for formally specifying strategies and propose simulation as a way to represent changes in the project environment.

Practical relevance

The results provide an analytical basis for a model of linked interactions involving both internal and external stakeholders. Such a model would support subsequent study of decisions about stakeholder management over the course of a project.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems
