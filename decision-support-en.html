Research

Intelligent decision support systems

Connecting data, models and computational procedures to management decisions.

I work on architectures in which data, simulations, artificial intelligence and optimization algorithms support the examination of management alternatives. Such systems need to make scenarios comparable and show how a recommendation follows from the underlying data.

Publications in this area cover enterprise digital ecosystems, service-oriented architecture, ontologies and decision support for oil and gas. Other work addresses the quality of input data and validation of the models used in these systems.
