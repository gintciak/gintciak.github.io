Comparative Analysis of Approaches to Developing Specialist Competencies in Innovative Industries

Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А. Сравнительный анализ подходов к развитию компетенций специалистов в инновационных отраслях промышленности // Неделя науки СПБПУ: материалы научной конференции с международным участием. 2019. С. 67–70.

Publication page

Research problem

Research-intensive enterprises need specialists who can use foundational knowledge and learn particular technologies as these evolve. The paper considers which competencies are best developed at university and which ones require participation in professional work.

Approach and methods

It compares university education with learning by doing. The analysis considers the nature of learning outcomes, the need for equipment and access to working conditions, and the differences among general, foundational professional and highly specialised competencies. A comparison table sets out the characteristics of the two approaches.

Findings

The author proposes combining the two kinds of training. Universities would focus on competencies that retain their value across technological changes. Enterprises would provide settings for learning specialised methods and equipment while carrying out real work. This division follows from a conceptual comparison of the approaches.

Practical relevance

The distinction can support discussions between programme designers and employers about how to organise placements and workplace learning. It also helps define the knowledge and skills students should acquire before they start training on a particular technology.
