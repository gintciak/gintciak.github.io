Models and Methods for Managing Research Projects Using Game Theory. Dissertation Summary

Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Research problem

This dissertation summary presents research in the Russian academic specialty 2.3.4, Management in Organizational Systems. It addresses how to represent interdependent decisions within a research project and assess changes to the rules that can move equilibrium outcomes closer to preferred outcomes.

Approach and methods

The document outlines the transition from an ontology of the project to a general game model and three problem-specific models. The method identifies participants, strategies and utilities and compares equilibrium and optimal profiles. The summary includes the principal assumptions and findings for the application cases.

Findings

The cases cover an individual’s decision to join a research team, a team’s choice of a proposal in a funding competition, and participation in exploratory work. The document describes the propositions put forward for defense and reports on the software implementation and trials in laboratory research projects. Assigning exploratory work to smaller groups is one of the measures discussed.

Practical relevance

The summary offers a compact account of the research contributions and the route from a general project model to an analysis of specific conflicts of interest. The full dissertation provides detailed derivations and experimental material.
