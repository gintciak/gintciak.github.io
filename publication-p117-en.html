Methods for Optimizing Enterprise Processes

Бекетов С.М., Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

This textbook covers the formulation and solution of optimization problems in enterprise operations. It is written for engineering, mathematics and economics students who need to translate a management question into a formal optimization problem.

Approach and methods

The book begins with the choice of decision variables, constraints and an objective function. It then presents linear and nonlinear methods, including the simplex and generalized reduced gradient methods. The treatment of discrete optimization includes greedy algorithms and dynamic programming for the knapsack problem. Chapters on stochastic and evolutionary search cover stochastic gradients, Monte Carlo methods, genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, particle swarms and ant colony optimization.

Findings

The multicriteria chapter addresses scalarization, the analytic hierarchy process and Pareto optimization. The final chapter organizes the choice of method around variable type, constraint structure, data uncertainty and practical requirements for precision, run time and interpretability.

Practical relevance

Exercises connect the formalization of production problems with the selection of a solution method. Readers can work through individual algorithm families or use the book to support an independent assignment that requires justifying the choice of an optimization approach.

Research areas

  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
  • Digital modelling of production systems

Keywords

process optimization, linear programming, discrete optimization, stochastic optimization, multicriteria optimization
