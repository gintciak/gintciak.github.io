Model for Stakeholders’ Strategic Interaction in the Scrum Methodology Project

Зубкова Д.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Зубкова Д.А., Гинцяк А.М. Модель стратегического взаимодействия заинтересованных сторон в проекте, реализуемом по методологии Scrum // Автоматизация и моделирование в проектировании и управлении. 2023. № 3 (21). С. 59–69.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

People involved in a Scrum project take decisions at different stages and assess outcomes in light of their own interests. This article develops a baseline model that links the order of moves to project stages and makes it possible to examine equilibrium decisions among stakeholders.

Approach and methods

Based on their literature review, the authors selected participants, Scrum stages and a representation for the interaction. They formulated the case as a dynamic game. The article sets out the assumptions and gives a mathematical and visual representation of the game. Its utility functions account for additional effort. The formulation covers three stakeholder types and three stages within one sprint.

Findings

The authors identified an equilibrium position for the game and conducted a numerical experiment. They show how to interpret the model output in terms of decisions taken by Scrum project participants. They also offer guidance for applying the base model and identify opportunities to add further parameters.

Practical relevance

The model gives a project manager a way to discuss how one participant’s decision alters the choices and expected outcome of others. With inputs adapted to a particular project, it can support comparison of scenarios based on different assumptions about stakeholder benefits and options.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems
