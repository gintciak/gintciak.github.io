Experimental Sample of a Software Module for Processing of a Cloud of Laser Scanning Points for Natural-Technical Systems Development

Bolsunovskaya M., Tammsaar S., Beliaevskii K., Fedotov A., Gintciak A.

Bibliographic citation

Bolsunovskaya M., Tammsaar S., Beliaevskii K., Fedotov A., Gintciak A. Publication page

Research problem

This conference paper concerns the processing of point clouds obtained by laser scanning natural and technical systems. It presents an experimental software module for that task. The paper is listed in the IBIMA proceedings and in the publication record published by the National Technology Initiative Centre at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems

Keywords

laser scanning, point cloud, spatial data processing, natural-technical systems
