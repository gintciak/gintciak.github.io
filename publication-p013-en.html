Regional Innovation System in Context of Pandemic COVID-19

Ekaterina Tereshko, Aleksei Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

Ekaterina Tereshko, Aleksei Gintciak. Regional Innovation System in Context of Pandemic COVID-19 // ACM International Conference Proceeding Series. ACM. 2020. pp. 1–7.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

The paper examines the channels through which the COVID-19 pandemic could affect regional innovation. A regional innovation system brings together the conditions for creating and subsequently commercialising knowledge and technology. The authors examine indirect effects transmitted through this system's external environment.

Approach and methods

The study uses political, economic, sociocultural, technological, environmental and legal analysis, commonly termed PESTEL. It groups regional innovation indicators into resources, outcomes of knowledge and technology creation, and commercialisation. Potential measures are selected from state statistics for later quantitative assessment. A qualitative diagram links the pandemic, the external factors and the regional innovation system.

Findings

The authors identify economic and sociocultural factors as channels of stronger indirect influence. They describe the political and technological channels as moderate and regard the environmental and legal channels as temporary in their qualitative assessment.

Practical relevance

The framework can structure follow-up studies of individual regions. It identifies measures and relationships that can be tested with quantitative data and helps determine which external changes to consider when analysing regional innovation policy.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords
