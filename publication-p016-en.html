Algorithm for Validation the Results of Computing Device Components’ Selecting

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Aleksey Gintciak, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksandra Loginova

Bibliographic citation

Marina Bolsunovskaya, Aleksey Gintciak, Svetlana Shirokova, Aleksandra Loginova. Algorithm for Validation the Results of Computing Device Components’ Selecting // E3S Web of Conferences. EDP Sciences. 2020. Vol. 217. Article 06011.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Computer-aided design can generate several feasible configurations for a computing device. Selecting among them requires assessing the requirements of individual components, operating conditions and properties of the complete device. This paper formalises the procedure for reviewing the generated design alternatives.

Approach and methods

The algorithm uses device and component data aligned in an ontological model. The assessment criteria are defined in relation to the requirements of the component. The authors consider structured expert assessment using PATTERN and the analytic hierarchy process with pairwise comparisons. They specify the required inputs, explain how to calculate individual and aggregate scores, and describe how to rank alternatives.

Findings

The algorithm returns a structured table. It associates each design option with criterion-level results and an aggregate assessment for the selected expert method. The procedure covers alternative implementations of an individual component as well as configurations of the complete device.

Practical relevance

The algorithm can be incorporated in a computer-aided synthesis system. Its tabular output helps designers review the constraints and criteria that influenced the ranking of an alternative.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

Keywords

computer-aided design, computing devices, multicriteria assessment, expert assessment, ontological model
