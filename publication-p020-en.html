Hybrid Simulation as a Key Tool for Socio-economic Systems Modeling

Aleksei M. Gintciak, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Alexandra A. Petryaeva

Bibliographic citation

Aleksei M. Gintciak, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Alexandra A. Petryaeva. Hybrid Simulation as a Key Tool for Socio-economic Systems Modeling // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems. Springer International Publishing. 2022. No. 442. pp. 262–272.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

The chapter examines the choice of modelling approach for socioeconomic systems in which processes operate at different scales and parameter values are uncertain. Its aim is to identify ways of combining models and assessing their suitability for decision support.

Approach and methods

The authors compare system dynamics, discrete-event simulation and agent-based modelling. They consider coupled, sequential, augmented and integrated ways of combining those paradigms. The review also considers methods for dealing with uncertainty and assessing models, including methods established in studies of technical systems.

Findings

It proposes two stages for selecting a model design. The choice of paradigms follows the modelling objectives. The choice of arrangement depends on the characteristics of the system. Approximately half of the papers examined combined system dynamics with discrete-event simulation. The authors also discuss sensitivity analysis, calibration, verification and validation.

Practical relevance

The review can inform the design and checking of a hybrid model for an applied study. It provides a basis for explaining which parts need detailed representation, how information should pass between them and which checks can be used to assess the resulting behaviour.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Multi-agent systems

Keywords

hybrid simulation, system dynamics, discrete-event simulation, agent-based modelling, model assessment
