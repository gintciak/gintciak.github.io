Research

Transport modelling and optimization

Models of air transport, urban routes and transport logistics.

In transport studies, I treat demand, capacity and operational constraints as interconnected parts of the model. In aviation this includes forecasting passenger flows and accounting for constraints when constructing schedules.

For public transit and logistics, I examine how decisions about routes, resources and sequences of operations affect service quality. The works collected here study transport as the primary system or as a substantial component of a wider one.
