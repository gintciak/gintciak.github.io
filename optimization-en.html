Research

Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

Finding trade-offs and assessing the stability of decisions with uncertain data.

Improving one performance measure often comes at a cost to another. I use Pareto sets to show the trade-offs available among completion times, cost, workload and reliability under a given set of constraints.

When inputs are stochastic, I examine how repeated evaluations affect decision quality. Another question is how to assess algorithm convergence and compare alternatives when the objective values themselves come from computational experiments.
