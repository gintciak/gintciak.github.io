Digitalization as a Way to Increase the Material Utilization Rate in Mechanical Engineering

Aleksei M. Gintciak, Mikhail B. Uspenskiy, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya

Bibliographic citation

Aleksei M. Gintciak, Mikhail B. Uspenskiy, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya. Digitalization as a Way to Increase the Material Utilization Rate in Mechanical Engineering // Communications in Computer and Information Science: International Conference for Information Systems and Design (ICID-2022). Springer Nature Switzerland. 2023. Vol. 1767. pp. 61–70.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Planning independently at several sites reduces opportunities to combine parts on the same cutting layouts. The chapter investigates how a discrete manufacturing company can relate its demand for parts to due dates, available production time and the processing requirements of different materials.

Approach and methods

The authors identified bottlenecks and interviewed company staff to determine what the algorithm had to support. They proposed combining part requirements across sites, preparing cutting layouts and allocating them to available production capacity. Timing is handled by successive approximations, with the last months of the planning horizon used as the starting point. The procedure concentrates production in earlier periods while retaining some flexibility to revise the plan.

Findings

The algorithm was implemented in software and tested on several production programs from the company. Combining requirements lets planners pack parts more closely on a cutting layout. Allocation is subject to due dates and working time, with production capacity reserved for urgent orders.

Practical relevance

The procedure connects decisions about material utilization with calendar planning. It can help a manufacturing company compare ways of consolidating requirements across sites when material consumption, on-time completion and the capacity to handle urgent jobs all matter.

Research areas

  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
  • Simulation-based optimization
