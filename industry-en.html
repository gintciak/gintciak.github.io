Research

Digital modelling of production systems

Production programmes, schedules, material flows and digital models of enterprises.

I study how to represent enterprise operations in a digital model when technological activities, people and equipment are linked by a shared plan. I pay particular attention to distributed production, where decisions about site workloads affect completion times and resource use.

Our publications address mechanical engineering, oil production and internal logistics. The model provides a setting for comparing production plans and process designs, with the structure of the actual enterprise defining the scope of the comparison.
