Decision-Making Algorithm for Optimizing Cargo Storage in 3PL Companies

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М. Алгоритм принятия решения об оптимизации размещения грузов на складе в компаниях 3PL-логистики // Sustainable development and engineering economics. 2025. Т. 4. С. 38–48.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

At a third-party logistics (3PL) warehouse, the sizes, weights, and mix of incoming cargo change over time. When space is used inefficiently, managers need to identify the cause. It may be related to how individual locations are filled, a shortage of available locations, or a mismatch between the rack layout and the cargo being handled.

Approach and methods

The authors define three related indicators. They measure the efficiency of location filling, overall location occupancy, and the suitability of the warehouse configuration. The third indicator represents the share of cargo for which positions with suitable dimensional and load characteristics are available. Decisions use target and critical levels for the indicators.

Findings

The paper presents a decision procedure that connects combinations of indicator values to actions such as consolidating existing loads or changing the layout. It discusses the experience of a 3PL operator that used storage audits and a dedicated warehouse management system report to collect the necessary data.

Practical relevance

The procedure can be used to review warehouse utilization regularly and identify when a layout change may be justified. Thresholds have to be set for the cargo mix and operating requirements of the individual site.
