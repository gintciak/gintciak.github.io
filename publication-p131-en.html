An Approach to Dynamic Project Planning in Organizational Systems Based on Deviation Analysis

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М. Подход к динамическому планированию проектов в организационных системах на основе анализа отклонений // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 211–215.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

An initial project schedule uses duration estimates that can be refined once tasks have been performed. This paper asks how observations about deviations can feed the next decisions on deadlines and resources through recurring schedule updates.

Approach and methods

A baseline plan is prepared from prior estimates. As work proceeds, actual durations, deviations and information about delays are recorded. Bayesian updating combines new observations with prior task duration distributions. Posterior estimates feed the next planning cycle, where an updated schedule is calculated.

Findings

The authors propose a feedback loop connecting initial planning, execution data and deviation analysis. The workflow shows where observations from monitoring enter the parameter estimation step and how the resulting estimates feed the next plan. The contribution is a specification of the workflow and the information it uses.

Practical relevance

The method can inform the design of a rescheduling tool that revises timelines as tasks finish and supports adjustments to resource allocation. Implementation requires deciding which observations to collect and when to recalculate the plan.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
