Managing an IT Project Team with a Network-Centric Model

Болсуновская М.В., Широкова С.В., Логинова А.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Болсуновская М.В., Широкова С.В., Логинова А.В., Гинцяк А.М. Управление командой ИТ-проекта на основе сетецентрической модели // Планирование и обеспечение подготовки кадров для промышленно-экономического комплекса региона. 2018. Т. 1. С. 179–183.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

The paper studies the organisation of a project team with several groups of specialists developing a system to predict failures in data storage. It asks how to examine working relationships alongside the management hierarchy and use both types of information to evaluate possible changes in team structure.

Approach and methods

The authors represent professional interactions as a graph. Network size and density describe overall connectivity, while degree centrality helps show how communication is distributed. Sociometric surveys and an assessment of team roles are proposed for studying interpersonal relations. The measures of network integrity and autonomy follow A. A. Denisov's information approach.

Findings

Each participant in the example has a working connection with at least one colleague. Several of the most highly connected nodes belong to people who coordinate project work. A comparison of the existing hierarchy with a calculated alternative illustrates how changing the number of assistants to the project manager affects the autonomy measure.

Practical relevance

The analysis can inform the design of working relationships in distributed teams. It supports discussions about centralisation and the allocation of coordination responsibilities.
