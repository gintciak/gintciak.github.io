Research summary · DOI

Research question

How can multi-agent methods balance labor across projects?

Research summary

Autonomous BDI project agents exchange resources and explore stochastic start times and Pareto trade-offs. A five-project experiment reports reductions of 7.7% in portfolio duration and 9.2% in labor effort.
