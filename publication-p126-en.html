Quality Criteria for Model-Based Managerial Decisions

Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М. Критерии качества модельно обоснованного управленческого решения // Стандарты и качество. 2026. № 11 (1073). С. 55–57.

Journal websiteeLIBRARY record

Research problem

The reliability of a managerial decision depends on the problem formulation, evidence, modeling assumptions and follow-up after implementation. The article develops criteria that can be checked across the preparation and practical use of decisions supported by computational models.

Approach and methods

The framework covers six groups of criteria. It examines the formulation, input data, adequacy of the model and constraints, coverage of evaluation measures, testing of alternatives and robustness, and feasibility and monitorability. Each group is tied to measurable indicators, such as the share of essential constraints captured in a model or discrepancies between modeled and observed results in retrospective testing.

Findings

The paper organizes a sequence of checks from the initial formulation to monitoring actual outcomes. Alternative analysis compares scenarios and tests whether the selected option retains its advantage when inputs vary. The feasibility check translates the modeled option into managerial actions and defines what to track during implementation.

Practical relevance

Organizations can incorporate the criteria into expert review and digital management procedures. Documenting assumptions, assessment results and the reasons for a choice makes the decision easier to revisit and audit.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords

managerial decision quality, digital model, alternative analysis, robustness, feasibility assessment
