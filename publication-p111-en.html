Application of a Probabilistic Schedule Estimation Model for Planning Software Development Projects

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М.

Bibliographic citation

Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М. Применение вероятностной модели расчёта сроков для планирования проектов разработки программного обеспечения // Известия Самарского научного центра Российской академии наук. 2026. Т. 28. № 3. С. 21–29.

Publication pageFull text

Research problem

Software project schedules are affected by uncertain task durations and competition for staff with the same roles. The study examines how the contingency allowed for individual tasks affects overall duration, the probability of meeting a project deadline, and time spent waiting for work to start.

Approach and methods

The project is represented as a directed graph whose nodes are tasks and whose edges are precedence relationships. Duration distributions are assigned to tasks. A scheduling percentile determines the durations used in the planned schedule. Monte Carlo simulation generates distributions of completion time and estimates staff idle time at different planning settings.

Findings

In the studied project, the authors imposed a deadline of 290 days and examined expected duration, the probability of meeting that deadline, and staff idle time. They observed a range of settings with similar probabilities followed by a sharp decline. The 74th percentile was selected as a compromise for preparing the schedule in that case.

Practical relevance

The method enables managers to compare plans by the risk of missing a deadline and the utilization of staff time. It requires information on task dependencies and staff roles, along with estimates of uncertainty in individual task durations.
