Digital Modelling of the System of Knowledge Exchange and Building Within a Network of Industrial Enterprises

Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Lo Thi Hong Van

Bibliographic citation

Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Lo Thi Hong Van. Digital Modelling of the System of Knowledge Exchange and Building Within a Network of Industrial Enterprises // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems: Digital Transformation: What is the Company of Today?. Springer Nature Switzerland. 2023. Vol. 805. pp. 35–44.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Knowledge exchange among industrial enterprises affects opportunities for collaborative research and development. Modeling the process requires a representation of both the choices of independent firms about network participation and the way their interactions change the stock of knowledge.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed strategies for forming networks of industrial firms and proposed combining agent-based simulation with system dynamics. The agent component represents independent companies and changes in the structure of their network. The system dynamics component represents the flows that build and transfer knowledge and their economic equivalents.

Findings

The chapter presents a conceptual hybrid model with feedback between the components. The results of the agent interactions determine coefficients for the knowledge-building processes. Changes in accumulated knowledge and its financial equivalent influence which strategies companies select. The feedback connects the way cooperation is organized with its expected benefits for individual firms.

Practical relevance

The design helps specify possible simulation experiments with different rules of collaboration and different network arrangements. Its representation of the feedback also identifies information needed to assess the consequences of sharing knowledge across firms.
