Game Theory Model of Research Team Formation

Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М. Теоретико-игровая модель формирования научного коллектива // Информатизация образования и науки. 2023. № 3 (59). С. 133–149.

Publication page

Research problem

A researcher’s decision to join a project depends on expected compensation, scientific benefits and required effort. When each prospective member makes this decision independently, the resulting team size can differ from the size that maximizes the team’s collective utility.

Approach and methods

The model represents prospective team members as symmetric agents choosing whether to take part. The benefit and effort of each participant depend on how many researchers join. The article studies the equilibrium number of participants, the size that maximizes collective utility, and the conditions under which either changes.

Findings

In the nondegenerate case of the model, the equilibrium team is at least as large as the collectively optimal one. The size of the gap depends on the model parameters. The author studies changes to total project benefits and limits on team size. Reducing the overall benefit can lead to a smaller equilibrium team while also reducing both individual and collective utilities.

Practical relevance

The model can be used to assess a maximum team size specified by a call for proposals. When examining a candidate cap, the paper suggests taking the characteristics of the project, including its complexity, into account and comparing the predicted composition with the collective optimum.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Project and portfolio management

Keywords

research team, game theory, team size, equilibrium, collective utility
