Research

Multi-agent systems

Models of decentralized control, coordination and self-organization.

Participants in a distributed system may have access to different resources and information, and may evaluate outcomes against different criteria. I model the rules of their interaction and study how local decisions affect schedules, capacity utilization and operational stability.

My collaborators and I investigate agent communication, production scheduling, self-organizing groups and decision support for oil and gas operations. Model experiments let us compare coordination rules under controlled conditions.
