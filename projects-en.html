Projects

I work with colleagues to build models that let us test management decisions before they are implemented. Some projects develop general modelling and optimisation methods. Others apply them to problems in manufacturing, transport and social policy.

Projects I lead or have led

  • Production scheduling with conflicting objectives

    2025–2026 · Russian Science Foundation · project 25-21-00322

    I am interested in which schedules remain acceptable when departments and sites pursue different objectives. We model a distributed production system as a set of interacting agents and investigate ways of coordinating local scheduling decisions.

    Russian Science Foundation project recordApplying a Multi-Agent Approach to Scheduling in Distributed Production SystemsDefinition and Formalization of the Relationships of the Structural Elements of Distributed Production Systems in the Context of the Task of Forming a ScheduleOn the Problem of Forming Sustainable Production Schedules in the Context of Conflicting Objective Functions of Management AgentsFormalization of the Scheduling Process in Distributed Production Systems as a Set of Strategic Interactions in Terms of Game Theory and Multi-Agent Systems

  • Multi-agent decision support systems in industry and construction

    2025–2026 · Priority 2030 · 075-15-2025-210 and 075-15-2026-097

    We study how to represent departmental decisions, individual objectives and shared process constraints in a single model. Multi-agent simulation lets us experiment with coordination and compare management scenarios.

    Project overviewAI-Supported Decision Making in Multi-Agent Production Systems Using the Example of the Oil and Gas IndustryThe Influence of Communication Strategies of Intelligent Agents in Production Systems on the Shift of Sustainable SolutionsIntelligent Decision Support System Based on Multi-Agent Interaction Models by the Example of the Oil and Gas Industry

  • Predictive and prescriptive analytics for industrial systems

    2024–2026 · State Research Assignment · FSEG-2024-0004 · 075-03-2024-004

    We study intelligent multi-agent systems that forecast production conditions and help justify management decisions. One question is how to connect a forecast to a choice of action whose effects can be evaluated in simulation.

    SPbPU research profileAI-Supported Decision Making in Multi-Agent Production Systems Using the Example of the Oil and Gas IndustryThe Influence of Communication Strategies of Intelligent Agents in Production Systems on the Shift of Sustainable SolutionsIntelligent Decision Support System Based on Multi-Agent Interaction Models by the Example of the Oil and Gas IndustryA Discrete-Event Model of a Parts Delivery System Using Transport Robots

  • Digital modelling of sociotechnical and production systems

    2021–2023 · State Research Assignment · FSEG-2021-0012 · 075-03-2021-050

    We developed methods and software for building digital models and evaluating management decisions. Illustrative applications covered production, regional development, tourism and social welfare. The project also produced a co-authored monograph on digital modelling.

  • Artificial intelligence for digital models of industrial and socioeconomic systems

    2023 · Priority 2030 · 075-15-2023-380

    We investigated how artificial intelligence can support data preparation, model development and validation. We examined applications with incomplete input data and a need to use models for scenario analysis.

    2025–2026 · Vladimir Potanin Foundation

    2023–2024 · Vladimir Potanin Foundation

    I developed game theory materials for master’s students studying innovation management. Students formalise interactions, solve game models and examine how their decisions change when the rules of the game change.

Applied work

  • Planning in discrete manufacturing

    In applied projects we use models to coordinate operations, equipment loading and the allocation of work across sites. Published examples address multi-stage production and the redistribution of production programmes.

    The Creation and Optimization of a Consolidated Production Program at Individual Machine-building EnterprisesHeuristic Approach to Planning Complex Multi-Stage Production SystemsHierarchical Approach to Production Planning of Machine-Building EnterprisesA Discrete-Event Model of a Parts Delivery System Using Transport Robots

  • Digital modelling for airlines

    In airline operations, I work on demand forecasting, resource allocation and schedule validation. These problems require models to account for both passenger flows and aircraft and crew constraints.

    SPbPU research profileDemand Forecasting Model for Airline Flights Based on Historical Passenger Flow Data

  • Production decisions in oil and gas

    My colleagues and I study modelling for oil production enterprises. Our publications cover production scheduling, resource allocation and decision support system architecture.

    Comprehensive Modeling of Oil Production Processes: An Analytical ReviewHierarchical Cybernetic Model of Oil Production Enterprise With Distributed Decision-Making CentersIntelligent Decision-Making Support System Architecture for Oil Production EnterprisesAI-Supported Decision Making in Multi-Agent Production Systems Using the Example of the Oil and Gas IndustryIntelligent Decision Support System Based on Multi-Agent Interaction Models by the Example of the Oil and Gas IndustryMulti-Objective Optimization of Labor Allocation in Surface Field Development Projects

  • Scenario analysis in social policy

    In a housing provision study for young people leaving care, we used a digital model to compare alternative processes and their consequences. The published article describes the model and its use.

    Modeling of Social Processes Welfare Using a Discrete-Event ApproachSocial Decision Support Based on a Digital Model
