Social Decision Support Based on a Digital Model

Боровков А.И., Незамаева О.Б., Болсуновская М.В., Елгина К.В., Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В.

Bibliographic citation

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Social policy authorities need to estimate how the level and allocation of funding affect the waiting list for housing. This article examines that problem for the Novosibirsk program that provides housing to orphans and people who grew up without parental care.

Approach and methods

The authors implemented a discrete-event model in AnyLogic. It represents options for purchasing apartments, issuing housing certificates and allocating funding to construction. The scenarios are compared by expenditure and the rate at which the queue declines. The experiments explore changes in both the total budget and the way it is divided among the options.

Findings

The paper reports a Pareto profile that allows scenarios to be compared at different budget levels. Under the study assumptions, a combination of construction, apartment purchase and housing certificates was a promising option. These channels received 40%, 40% and 20% of the available allocation, respectively, in the model scenario. The preferred policy depended on the amount of funding available.

Practical relevance

The model allows the implications of proposed allocations to be explored before they are implemented. The authors identify demographic trends and changes in the housing market as additional factors for long-term forecasting. The numerical results are specific to the assumptions used in the study.
