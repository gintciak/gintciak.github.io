Practical Problems of Continuous Production Process Management

Рыбакова Ю.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Рыбакова Ю.В., Гинцяк А.М. Практические проблемы управления непрерывными производственными процессами // Управление качеством продукции на основе передовых производственных технологий: Сборник тезисов докладов VI Международного форума "Передовые цифровые и производственные технологии". 2024. С. 72–77.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

In continuous production, changes in one part of the material flow can affect equipment utilization, product quality and downstream operations. The authors investigate recurring management problems and examine the links between their causes and operational consequences.

Approach and methods

The analysis draws on a study of an enterprise in the mining and metallurgical sector. The authors organize the circumstances and consequences of each identified problem in tables. They consider variability in incoming raw materials, diagnostic lead times, coordination of production schedules, internal logistics and monitoring at intermediate stages.

Findings

The study identifies three groups of problems involving data on raw material quality, synchronization between stages and intermediate monitoring. The authors link these problems to difficulties in adjusting operating conditions, as well as downtime, uneven loads and accumulation of work in progress. The analysis identifies high-level causes that would need further investigation for the processes at a given enterprise.

Practical relevance

The tables can guide industrial process reviews by identifying the data to gather and the dependencies to examine first. The study offers a basis for discussing changes to information exchange, input and intermediate quality checks, and coordination of production schedules.
