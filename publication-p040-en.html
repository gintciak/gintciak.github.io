Digital Tool for Modeling the Index of Innovative Development of Regions

Жидков Д., Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Жидков Д., Гинцяк А. Цифровой инструмент моделирования индекса инновационного развития регионов // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: Материалы Всероссийской студенческой учебно-научной конференции. 2023. С. 20–23.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

The paper addresses a task within a digital platform for regional innovation analysis. The proposed module estimates a regional innovation index when its underlying factors change, giving users a way to examine the implications of alternative sets of input values.

Approach and methods

Seven groups of predictors are considered. Four correspond to businesses, research organizations, universities and government. Three describe economic conditions, social conditions and digitalization. After reviewing data analysis tools, the authors chose multiple linear regression and fitted its coefficients by least squares using data for 2017–2019.

Findings

The resulting software tool calculates the index for a set of indicators supplied by the user. With the weights fitted to observations, the user can vary the inputs and recompute the index without re-estimating all coefficients for each scenario. The authors acknowledge the limitations of a linear specification and identify alternative prediction models and further analysis of the contribution of individual factors as future development.

Practical relevance

The module supports comparison of scenarios in terms of the indicators that change and the estimated index values. Bringing this calculation into the platform connects regional data with a repeatable evaluation procedure that can be rerun when new observations become available.
