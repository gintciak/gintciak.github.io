Identification of a Model for Solving Management Problems in Organizational Systems in Bounded Rationality of Control Subjects Conditions

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М. Идентификация модели для решения задач управления в организационных системах в условиях ограниченной рациональности субъектов управления // Вестник НГУЭУ. 2026. № 2. С. 29–50.

Publication page

Research problem

In decentralized control, departments can hold different information about the same system and disagree on the changes they want to achieve. The paper asks how to identify a control model that can represent their objectives, priorities and bounded rationality.

Approach and methods

The procedure begins by identifying the system and its decision makers. Expert assessments supplement current and retrospective data. The control object is represented by attributes in a multidimensional state space. Objectives have a vector representation, and priority coefficients support their aggregation when joint interventions are considered. The authors discuss the Delphi method as a means of reducing the effect of individual cognitive limitations on elicited judgments.

Findings

A shipbuilding example demonstrates the procedure for decision makers with different objectives and shows how conflicts of interest can be represented formally. Some parameters in the example are illustrative. The paper specifies the steps of model identification and explains how ranges in the state space can represent uncertainty about the assessed attributes.

Practical relevance

The approach can inform the design of decision support models and digital twins that need to capture responsibilities, priorities and information sources. Quantitative estimation of the behavioral parameters requires further work with enterprise data.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Multi-agent systems

Keywords

organizational systems, bounded rationality, model identification, expert elicitation, decentralized control
