Definition and Formalization of the Relationships of the Structural Elements of Distributed Production Systems in the Context of the Task of Forming a Schedule

Бурлуцкая Ж., Шарко П., Гинцяк А., Поспелов К.

Bibliographic citation

Бурлуцкая Ж., Шарко П., Гинцяк А., Поспелов К. Определение и формализация связей структурных элементов распределённых производственных систем в контексте задачи формирования расписания // Sustainable development and engineering economics. 2025. Т. 3. С. 41–65.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Participants in a distributed production system have access to different information, evaluate outcomes by different criteria, and exercise different decision rights. A scheduling model has to relate the organizational structure to communication and to the objectives of the people or units involved. This study formalizes these relationships under uncertainty and decentralized control.

Approach and methods

The authors review work indexed in Scopus and the Russian Science Citation Index. They describe production roles, exchanges of information, and the revision of schedules. Game theory provides a way to define strategies and payoffs. A belief, desire, and intention architecture (BDI) is used to specify the behavior of agents with different responsibilities.

Findings

The paper presents ontologies of the communication network and the scheduling process. It describes two patterns of strategic interaction. Under a bottom-up pattern, an initiative begins with agents at lower levels. Under a top-down pattern, the interaction starts from an assignment or decision at a higher management level. The frameworks identify participants' roles and formalize their strategies.

Practical relevance

These representations provide a basis for planning support systems that compare scheduling scenarios and examine how local objectives affect coordination of the production plan.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction

  • Intelligent decision support systems
