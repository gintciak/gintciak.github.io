A Framework for Optimizing Human Resource Allocation in Hierarchical Project Portfolio Management

Бекетов С.М., Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Бекетов С.М., Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М. Принципиальный подход к оптимизации распределения трудовых ресурсов при иерархическом управлении портфелем проектов // Интеллектуальная инженерная экономика и Индустрия 5.0 (ЭКОПРОМ): Сборник трудов международной научно-практической конференции. 2024. С. 557–562.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

A set of concurrent projects may need the same specialist at different times, while project completion depends on the sequence of work within each project. The paper asks how to coordinate resource allocation at these two levels and compare staffing alternatives in terms of duration and labour requirements.

Approach and methods

At project level, a network diagram is built from information on tasks, precedence and work requirements. Candidate teams are generated stochastically, their durations and workloads are estimated and nondominated alternatives are identified. At portfolio level, combinations of candidate teams are assessed considering concurrent schedules and shared resources. A further Pareto comparison is performed using the resulting portfolio metrics.

Findings

The result is a two-stage process design showing the inputs, calculations and handover between the project and portfolio levels. Its structure describes how results from a local optimization feed into the assessment of an entire portfolio. Portfolio formation and dynamic reallocation while projects are under way are identified as further work.

Practical relevance

The design can inform staffing tools that present managers with several defensible alternatives. Network models make the implications for individual projects visible, and the portfolio calculation can incorporate competition for shared employees.
