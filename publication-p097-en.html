Development of a Typical Life Cycle Model of a Knowledge-Intensive Project at Oil and Gas Industry Enterprises

Абрамова А., Гинцяк А., Редько С., Лундаева К.

Bibliographic citation

Абрамова А., Гинцяк А., Редько С., Лундаева К. Типовая модель жизненного цикла наукоёмкого проекта на предприятиях нефтегазовой отрасли // Sustainable development and engineering economics. 2025. Т. 1. С. 80–94.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

The paper studies how to estimate the duration of a knowledge-intensive project in the oil and gas industry. Completion depends on relationships among stages from requirements analysis through implementation and ongoing support. Forecasting that duration requires an account of sector-specific procedures and how they interact within the life cycle.

Approach and methods

The authors analyze project stages and use system dynamics to represent their causal relationships. A conceptual stock and flow diagram captures the links among processes and state variables. Functional relationships then specify how those variables evolve, enabling the model to trace changes in project conditions over time.

Findings

The resulting simulation model covers the main stages of project delivery and includes audit as a supporting process. It can estimate completion time conditional on the parameters specified for the enterprise being studied. The authors identify the representation of investment risks and quantitative estimates of their likelihood as future extensions.

Practical relevance

For project managers, the model provides a way to examine which processes contribute to delays and how the overall duration estimate changes with the assumptions. Application to a specific enterprise requires configuring the model parameters and processes for its operating procedures.
