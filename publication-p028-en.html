Possibilities of Applying a Hybrid Approach to Modeling Socio-Economic and Sociotechnical Systems

Болсуновская М., Гинцяк А., Бурлуцкая Ж., Петряева А., Зубкова Д., Успенский М., Селедцова И.

Research problem

Socioeconomic and sociotechnical systems involve processes at different scales. A model may need to represent aggregate flows, discrete events and the behaviour of individual participants. This review examines how researchers can choose modelling paradigms and decide how the components should interact.

Approach and methods

The authors classify hybrid models by combinations of system dynamics, discrete-event simulation and agent-based modelling. A separate classification concerns the way the components are combined. The paper discusses coupled, sequential, augmented and integrated designs and illustrates how components can exchange information or jointly represent related processes.

Findings

Approximately half of the reviewed examples combine system dynamics with discrete-event simulation. The authors recommend choosing paradigms in response to the objectives, then choosing the structure of the combined model in light of the system being studied. They also consider model assessment methods that can carry over from the study of technical systems.

Practical relevance

The classification gives applied researchers a basis for explaining the structure of a hybrid model, its required level of detail and the information that must pass between its components.

Research areas

  • Multi-agent systems

  • Intelligent decision support systems
