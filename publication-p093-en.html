Modern Optimization Methods and Their Application Features

Бекетов С.М., Зубкова Д.А., Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Редько С.Г.

Bibliographic citation

Бекетов С.М., Зубкова Д.А., Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Редько С.Г. Современные методы оптимизации и особенности их применения // Russian Technological Journal. 2025. Т. 13. № 4. С. 78–94.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

The review organizes optimization methods and computational tools for model development. Algorithm selection depends on the mathematical formulation, properties of the objective function, the constraints, and the capabilities of the software environment. The paper provides a starting point for determining which of those considerations matter for a particular study.

Approach and methods

The authors draw on research publications, analytical materials, and technical documentation. They consider established and more recent optimization methods, including approaches that work with explicit mathematical formulations and approaches for searching over alternatives evaluated through simulation. The comparison is based on problem characteristics, how the methods work, and applications described in the literature.

Findings

The review connects classes of optimization problems to algorithms and the tools available for implementing them. It discusses advantages, limitations, and suitable areas of application, linking theoretical requirements to implementation choices. The resulting classification supports the selection of candidates for computational evaluation. Experiments comparing methods across several models are identified as further work.

Practical relevance

The paper can help developers of mathematical and simulation models anticipate which properties of a problem will constrain algorithm choice, identify tools suitable for the required calculations, and choose what to compare when they test alternative implementations.

Research areas

  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

optimization methods, literature review, computational tools, simulation modeling, algorithm selection
