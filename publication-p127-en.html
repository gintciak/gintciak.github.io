Research summary · DOI

Research question

How can computational costs be reduced when configuring and optimizing a simulation model?

Research summary

Gaussian-process surrogate modeling with adaptive sampling is implemented as a simulation wrapper. In a two-parameter bus-route case, errors stabilized after about 100–120 samples; passenger-flow R² reached 0.999.
