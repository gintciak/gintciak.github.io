An Approach to the Configuration and Optimization of Simulation Models of Organizational Systems

Поспелов К.Н., Лундаева К.А., Бекетов С.М., Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Поспелов К.Н., Лундаева К.А., Бекетов С.М., Дергачев М.В., Гинцяк А.М. Подход к конфигурации и оптимизации имитационных моделей организационных систем // Современные наукоемкие технологии. 2026. № №10 2026. С. 141–149.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Scenario analysis with stochastic simulation can require many expensive runs as the number of control parameters increases. The paper investigates how to reduce this cost while searching for effective configurations.

Approach and methods

In the proposed loop, simulation runs provide training data for a Gaussian process surrogate. The optimizer uses the surrogate to evaluate candidate control settings. New runs are allocated to regions where approximation accuracy is insufficient. Particle swarm search helps identify where to collect additional data. Root mean squared error and the coefficient of determination are used to assess predictive accuracy.

Findings

The authors tested the approach on a discrete-event model of a single public transport route. Across the evaluated measures, approximation errors became broadly stable after about 100 to 120 training points. The paper reports fewer simulation runs than exhaustive search and comparable configuration quality. The test used a single model with two control parameters.

Practical relevance

The architecture can wrap an existing simulator by exchanging control settings and output measures. Further experiments are needed to evaluate higher-dimensional applications and compare alternative optimizers.
