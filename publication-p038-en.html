Comprehensive Modeling of Oil Production Processes: An Analytical Review

Болсуновская М., Гинцяк А., Федяевская Д., Петряева А., Бурлуцкая Ж.

Bibliographic citation

Болсуновская М., Гинцяк А., Федяевская Д., Петряева А., Бурлуцкая Ж. Комплексное моделирование процессов нефтедобычи: аналитический обзор // Автоматизация и информатизация ТЭК. 2023. № 2. С. 51–62.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

Production-rate forecasting, technological accounting and operating analysis concern different levels of an oil production system. A pump model and a field-level model require different data and represent different processes. This review organizes those distinctions to help select an approach for a particular task.

Approach and methods

The authors compared published models using six criteria. They considered the modeling level from the pump to the global scale, the type of model and its tools, support for decisions at multiple levels, testing on real fields, and the intended task. The approaches covered analytical and simulation models, statistical methods and machine learning.

Findings

The review relates groups of methods to their intended applications and forecasting horizons. It discusses how the features represented in the input data affect the applicability of statistical and machine-learning models. The authors identify potential benefits of simulation models that represent reservoir structure and geology, and discuss the effort required to develop a hydrodynamic model.

Practical relevance

The classification helps engineers identify published studies relevant to a decision, assess their data requirements and check whether they have been evaluated on field data. It provides a basis for defining further studies of production processes.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

oil production, simulation modeling, production rate forecasting, multilevel models, analytical review
