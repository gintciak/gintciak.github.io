Modeling of Domestic Tourism: Analysis of Models and Their IT Support

Бурлуцкая Ж., Петряева А., Гинцяк А., Сидорина Е.

Bibliographic citation

Бурлуцкая Ж., Петряева А., Гинцяк А., Сидорина Е. Моделирование внутреннего туризма: анализ моделей и их IT-поддержки // Фундаментальные и прикладные исследования в области управления, экономики и торговли: Сборник трудов Всероссийской научно-практической и учебно-методической конференции. 2021. Publication pageFull text in the proceedings

Research problem

Forecasting visitor numbers and assessing the effects of investment in tourism infrastructure call for different models. This review examines studies of demand, traveller behaviour and the effects of investment on the tourism sector. Its aim is to identify promising methods for analysing domestic tourism.

Approach and methods

The papers are grouped by modelling approach and input data. The examples include panel data models, vector autoregression, structural equation modelling, applied general equilibrium models and deep learning. Plog's typology is discussed as a way to represent travellers' motivations. The review considers sources ranging from arrival counts and economic measures to online searches.

Findings

The authors relate model selection to the purpose of the study. They identify structural equation modelling as useful for examining factors that influence demand and general equilibrium models for analysing industry-level effects of investment. They also discuss the value of econometric methods for projections based on historical observations.

Practical relevance

The review can guide data collection and model selection according to whether the decision requires a demand forecast, a behavioural account or the evaluation of an infrastructure scenario.
