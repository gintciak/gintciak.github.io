Journal article · 2023

Modeling the Participation of the Research Team in the Competition for the Implementation of a Research Project

Гинцяк А.М., Редько С.Г.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М., Редько С.Г. Моделирование участия научного коллектива в конкурсе на реализацию научно-исследовательского проекта // Информатизация образования и науки. 2023. № 4 (60). С. 117–131.

Publication page

Research problem

The rules of a grant competition affect the choices of research teams and the expected effectiveness of funding. The paper examines a contest in which both the funding organization and competing teams make decisions in pursuit of their own objectives.

Approach and methods

The authors represent the competition as a strategic game and combine analytical study with a corresponding simulation. The simulation compares competitions with one and two winners across different numbers of participating research teams. The outcomes include the median and quartiles of the estimated effectiveness of funding.

Findings

In the experiments, the statistics changed as more teams entered the competition and then approached saturation. The scenario with two winners had higher funding effectiveness in the model. The difference between the two scenarios ranged from 2.5% to 10% across the conditions considered. In the experiment, it narrowed to 2.5–3.0% when at least 50 teams participated.

Practical relevance

The combined model can support initial assessment of rules such as the number of awards, the maximum budget and winner selection criteria. The reported effects are specific to the contest structure and parameter settings evaluated in the study.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Simulation-based optimization

Keywords

research grant competitions, grant funding, game-theoretic model, simulation modeling, research teams
