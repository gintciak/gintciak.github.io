Multiparametric Optimization of Complex System Management Scenarios Based on Simulation Models

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Konstantin D. Troshchenko

Bibliographic citation

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Konstantin D. Troshchenko. Multiparametric Optimization of Complex System Management Scenarios Based on Simulation Models // International Journal of Technology. 2023. Vol. 14. No. 8. pp. 1748–1758.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Searching for management decisions with a simulation model requires repeated scenario evaluations. As the number of interdependent inputs grows, so does the cost of the experiment. This study asks how scenarios can be selected to provide useful coverage within a limited computation budget.

Approach and methods

The authors formalize the selection of a scenario set and discuss scenario reduction and evolutionary search as ways to refine the method. Their prototype groups dependent inputs and randomly selects combinations consistent with the constraints of each group. A simulation module evaluates the scenario and the resulting point is compared with the current approximation of the Pareto front.

Findings

The main result is an iterative optimization procedure illustrated on a problem with two objectives. The prototype uses a specified number of iterations as its stopping condition. Comparative tests of evolutionary methods and experiments with real data are identified as subsequent research.

Practical relevance

The formulation is relevant to decision-support tools where each simulation run is expensive. It shows how admissible scenario generation, simulation runs and the accumulation of trade-off solutions can fit into a single computational process.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

simulation optimization, scenario evaluation, multiparametric optimization, Pareto front, random scenario generation
