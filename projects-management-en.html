Research

Project and portfolio management

Team formation, resource allocation and schedule management.

I study how to assign staff to projects and coordinate decisions across levels of portfolio management. Some problems call for matching skills to work requirements, while others involve uncertain activity durations and competition for shared staff.

My work brings together game theory, multi-objective optimization, probabilistic planning and multi-agent models. Examples come from research, manufacturing and software projects.
