Conference paper · 2019

Video Lections Intellectual Analysis Software Module Development

Gintciak A., Beliaevskii K., Fomina M., Loginova A., Somov Ya.

Bibliographic citation

Gintciak A., Beliaevskii K., Fomina M., Loginova A., Somov Ya. Video Lections Intellectual Analysis Software Module Development // Proceedings of the 33rd International Business Information Management Association Conference, IBIMA 2019: Education Excellence and Innovation Management through Vision 2020. 2019. pp. 8852–8859.

Publication page

Research problem

The paper addresses time-consuming parts of producing lecture videos. These include detecting objects within a frame, aligning a lecture recording with the slide deck, and preparing the processed footage for further editing.

Approach and methods

The module architecture includes an interface, a scheduler, video processing workers, a database and file storage. The scheduler assigns jobs to available workers. The pipeline converts the presentation to images, detects the screen and speaker, replaces the recorded projection with the source slide, and produces output files. The paper also describes the classes used to store job status and manage assignment to workers.

Findings

For studio recordings, the authors report real-time recognition and classification accuracy of at least 98.2% for the main objects under the conditions specified in the paper. They demonstrate support for colour correction, editing layers and a separate mask for the lecturer.

Practical relevance

The architecture can integrate automated processing into an online lecture production workflow. Export to an Adobe Premiere project gives an editor access to the processed material and allows further work in an existing editing environment.
