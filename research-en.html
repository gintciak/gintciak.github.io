Research

Across these ten areas, I study how to model complex systems and compare decisions through computational experiments.

  • Simulation-based optimization

    Using models to test interventions and find decisions that perform acceptably across scenarios.

  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

    Finding trade-offs and assessing the stability of decisions with uncertain data.

  • Multi-agent systems

    Models of decentralized control, coordination and self-organization.

  • Game theory and strategic interaction

    Strategy choice, incentives and interactions among stakeholders.

  • Project and portfolio management

    Team formation, resource allocation and schedule management.

  • Digital modelling of production systems

    Production programmes, schedules, material flows and digital models of enterprises.

  • Intelligent decision support systems

    Connecting data, models and computational procedures to management decisions.

  • Transport modelling and optimization

    Models of air transport, urban routes and transport logistics.

  • Modelling socioeconomic systems

    Regional development, tourism and the provision of public services.

  • Intellectual property management

    Modelling patent strategies, managing portfolios and analysing relationships across assets.
