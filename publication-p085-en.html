Modern Approaches in Systems Engineering and Digital Modelling of Complex Production Systems

Editorial board member · А. М. Гинцяк

Bibliographic citation

Современные подходы в системном инжиниринге и цифровом моделировании сложных производственных систем (SEDM-2025), 27-28 февраля 2025 года. Санкт-Петербург: ПОЛИТЕХ-ПРЕСС. Редакционная коллегия А. М. Гинцяк. 2025. 527 с. ISBN 978-5-7422-9043-8.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

This volume presents work from the first national conference on systems engineering and digital modelling of complex production systems, held at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University on 27 and 28 February 2025. Contributions consider tools for designing and studying complex systems, assessing operating scenarios and supporting management decisions.

Approach and methods

Six sections cover production, transport and economic systems, complex technical devices, medicine and social services, and educational technologies. The papers present modelling problems from several fields, including production planning, assessment of technological processes, engineering design and digital decision support.

Findings

The volume was published by POLYTECH-PRESS with ISBN 978-5-7422-9043-8. Marina Bolsunovskaya served as executive editor. I was a member of the editorial board and contributed to individual papers as a co-author.

Practical relevance

The collection provides examples of applied systems engineering studies. Its thematic sections allow readers to compare how authors in different fields formulate problems, record assumptions and select calculations for investigating complex systems.
