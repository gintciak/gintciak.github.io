The Creation and Optimization of a Consolidated Production Program at Individual Machine-building Enterprises

Бурлуцкая Ж., Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Бурлуцкая Ж., Гинцяк А. Создание и оптимизация консолидированной производственной программы на предприятиях дискретного машиностроения // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: Материалы Всероссийской студенческой учебно-научной конференции. 2022. С. 43–48.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

A machine-building enterprise plans laser and plasma cutting at several production sites. The paper asks whether pooling orders would make it possible to pack parts more densely on sheets and use available capacity more effectively while continuing to accommodate urgent jobs.

Approach and methods

The study draws on interviews with enterprise representatives and an analysis of business processes and bottlenecks. Low material utilisation is linked to planning each site separately, the limited variety of parts in individual batches and the insertion of urgent jobs without recalculating the production plan. The authors also identify a need for common equipment, material and process reference data.

Findings

The work specifies requirements for an order-allocation algorithm. These include preparing a consolidated plan, calculating available capacity for machines and workshops, and assigning work to sites and time periods in line with due dates. The authors give a preliminary estimate of a possible 2–8% improvement in material utilisation, depending on the metal and production programme.

Practical relevance

The requirements can guide the design of an information system for production consolidation. Applying them elsewhere calls for accounting for local equipment, product ranges and logistics.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization
