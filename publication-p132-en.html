An Approach to Payoff Distribution among Participants of a Self-Organizing Workgroup

Шарко П.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Шарко П.А., Гинцяк А.М. Подход к распределению выигрышей участников самоорганизующейся рабочей группы // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 215–220.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

At the early stages of a work group's life cycle, members choose whether to join and which roles to take. The promised share of the group payoff affects those choices. This paper formalizes that initial payoff allocation during group formation and conflict.

Approach and methods

The problem is stated in cooperative game theory. A participant can take multiple roles, so the contribution calculation considers admissible role bundles and coalition memberships. The allocation rule builds on the Shapley value, which assigns a payoff based on the expected marginal contribution made when a coalition forms.

Findings

The authors propose a formulation of the Shapley value that accounts for feasible role assignments. It connects initial payoff allocation to decisions about joining a coalition and taking roles during the early stages of group development. Constructing the sets of admissible configurations is left for further work.

Practical relevance

The rule provides a basis for comparing team compositions in a decision support model. Applying it requires defining participants' available roles, valuing different coalitions and representing individual objectives.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Multi-agent systems
  • Project and portfolio management

cooperative game theory, Shapley value, payoff allocation, self-organization, work group
