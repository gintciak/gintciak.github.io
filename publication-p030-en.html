Development of a High-Level Demographic Model of the Region

Федяевская Д., Гинцяк А.

Bibliographic citation

Федяевская Д., Гинцяк А. Разработка укрупнённой демографической модели региона // Цифровая трансформация экономических систем: проблемы и перспективы (ЭКОПРОМ–2022). Сборник трудов Всероссийской научно-практической конференции с зарубежным участием. 2022. С. 743–745.

Publication page

Research problem

Regional demographic models help assess changes in population size and structure over the long term. These changes affect the expected demand for public services. This paper selects a modelling approach for an aggregate regional study and considers what can be represented using available data.

Approach and methods

The authors review work on population forecasting, migration, births and mortality. The comparison covers research objectives, simulation paradigms and indicators. It also examines how modelling migration depends on having sufficiently detailed and complete information about the age and sex structure of flows.

Findings

The paper identifies system dynamics as an appropriate choice for an aggregate model that can compare scenarios. It discusses the importance of migration for long-term population projections and the problems that arise when detailed flow information is incomplete. Agent-based approaches are also represented in the reviewed examples.

Practical relevance

The study establishes a basis for developing a regional model that can be linked to policy questions. Selecting the indicators in advance can help plan studies of demand for educational facilities, social benefits and other public services.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

regional demography, system dynamics, simulation modelling, migration flows, social planning
