A Modular Approach to Teaching Digital Modeling of Production Processes

Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н. Модульный подход к организации преподавания цифрового моделирования производственных процессов // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 325–329.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

The same subject in digital production modeling can be taught in specialist degree programs, elective modules and professional development. This paper addresses how teaching materials can be organized to suit the students and purpose of each program.

Approach and methods

The course is divided into a shared core and optional modules. The theory covers the role of models in decision making, classes of models and modeling stages. Practical modules develop skills in building and studying models. An advanced module uses industrial cases where students select a formulation and tool for a particular problem.

Findings

The authors specify how the configuration depends on the audience, degree program, prior preparation, available time, teaching format and modeling tools. They present a shared sequence from problem formulation and model development through experimental work to interpretation. The sequence can support different selections of practical assignments.

Practical relevance

The structure can guide the design of a coordinated set of teaching materials and exercises for multiple versions of the course. It provides a basis for selecting applications and deciding how deeply to cover each topic.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

digital modeling, production processes, modular teaching, course materials, model-based decision making
