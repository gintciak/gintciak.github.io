A Systematic Approach to the Development of Hybrid Models Based on the Integration of Artificial Intelligence and Simulation Modeling

Нигматулин А.Д., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н., Гинцяк А.М.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

Combining simulation and artificial intelligence calls for decisions about what each component should do, how the components exchange data and how their joint behavior will be evaluated. This paper covers the development cycle of a hybrid model, including reproducibility and scaling.

Approach and methods

The approach begins with problem formulation and the assignment of responsibilities to simulation and machine learning. The authors discuss sequential, parallel and ensemble arrangements, the preparation of observed and synthetic data, and the use of surrogates for expensive computation. Validation includes comparisons with stand-alone components. The paper also considers interpretation of outputs and documentation of the development stages.

Findings

The paper presents a design sequence for selecting an architecture, integrating models, validating the result and scaling up. A public transport route serves as an example of combining a discrete-event model of operations with an optimization component.

Practical relevance

The workflow is relevant when outputs from simulation experiments feed model training or decision search. It helps teams specify the data their components require and decide in advance how to evaluate their combined use.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Transport modelling and optimization
