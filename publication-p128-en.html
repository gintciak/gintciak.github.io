Innovation Management in the Context of Digital Transformation

Executive editor · А. М. Гинцяк

Bibliographic citation

Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции (IQ-2026), 17–18 апреля 2026 года. Санкт-Петербург: ПОЛИТЕХ-ПРЕСС. Ответственный редактор А. М. Гинцяк. 2026. 504 с. ISBN 978-5-7422-9676-8.

Conference website

Research problem

This volume collects contributions to the Fifth National Scientific and Practical Conference on Innovation Management in the Context of Digital Transformation, held at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University on 17 and 18 April 2026. A. M. Gintciak is identified as executive editor.

Approach and methods

The contributions are grouped around innovation projects and products, management of organizational systems, mathematical and computer models for decision support, and quality management.

Findings

The proceedings present applied problem formulations, models and methods for industry, research projects and educational practice. Contributors include students, doctoral researchers, academic staff and industry practitioners. Individual authored papers are described in their own bibliographic records.

Practical relevance

The volume is a reference for work on control in organizational systems, product quality management and production organization, systems analysis and information processing, and automation and control of technological processes. It was issued by POLYTECH-PRESS as a 504-page volume, ISBN 978-5-7422-9676-8.
