Statement of the Problem of Managing Intellectual Property Objects in Knowledge-Intensive Enterprises

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М. Постановка задачи управления объектами интеллектуальной собственности в наукоёмких предприятиях // Управление инновациями в условиях цифровой трансформации: сборник трудов V Всероссийской научно-практической конференции. 2026. С. 234–239.

Conference proceedings

Research problem

In a long research and development cycle, decisions about legal protection and use of intellectual output depend on earlier decisions about how work is planned and organized. The authors examine intellectual property management as part of a knowledge-intensive enterprise's broader research management system.

Approach and methods

The structural and functional model links objectives, inputs, outputs and control actions to stages in the life cycle of intellectual outputs. It covers planning, creation, legal protection, use and commercialization. It also accounts for decision levels and external requirements, including legal protection, technology readiness and the state of the art.

Findings

The result is a closed-loop model with feedback. Information about outputs that have been created and used, along with knowledge and experience accumulated in earlier projects, can feed the planning of subsequent research. The model shows how intellectual property processes relate to the enterprise's research and development management functions.

Practical relevance

The model can support the definition of departmental responsibilities and information exchanges, and it can inform the design of systems that track intellectual outputs at different stages of their life cycle.

Research areas

  • Intellectual property management
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

intellectual property, research life cycle, structural and functional model, feedback, legal protection
