Honours

I began my academic career at SPbPU, where I studied, defended my dissertation, began teaching and took on the leadership of a digital modelling laboratory. This page brings together the main appointments, awards and professional roles.

Education and appointments at SPbPU

  • Associate Professor at the Higher School of Project Activity and Industrial Innovation

    Since 2024

    I teach mathematical modelling and management methods. Since 2026 I have directed the master’s programme in mathematical and computer modelling for high-tech production management.

    Master’s programme page

  • Candidate of Technical Sciences

    SPbPU · speciality 2.3.4 Control in Organizational Systems · 2024

    My dissertation examined how game theory can support research project management, including team formation and incentives. I defended it in November 2023 and received the Candidate of Sciences degree in 2024.

    Dissertation at SPbPUModels and Methods for Managing Research Projects Using Game TheoryModels and Methods for Managing Research Projects Using Game Theory. Dissertation Summary

  • Head of the Digital Modelling of Industrial Systems Laboratory

    Advanced Engineering School of Digital Engineering · since 2021

    I lead the laboratory’s work on models, methods and software for industrial and organisational systems.

    Laboratory announcement

  • Research in stream data processing laboratories

    SPbPU · since 2017 · lead research fellow since 2024

    I started as an engineer in the Stream Data Processing Algorithms and Systems Laboratory and moved to engineering and research roles in the Industrial Stream Data Processing Systems Laboratory. My work includes operations research, simulation and the analysis of strategic interactions in organisational systems.

    Laboratory profile

  • Teaching assistant and senior lecturer

    SPbPU · 2019–2022, 2022–2024

    I taught practical classes in project work, operations research and game theory. I also coordinated student research and supervised undergraduate and master’s theses.

  • Undergraduate, master’s and doctoral studies

    SPbPU · 2013–2023

    I completed a bachelor’s (2013–2017) and a master’s (2017–2019) in Innovation Studies, followed by doctoral studies in Computer Science and Engineering (2019–2023).
