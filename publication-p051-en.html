Complex Modelling of Regional Tourism Systems

Gintciak A., Burlutskaya Z., Zubkova D., Petryaeva A.

Bibliographic citation

Gintciak A., Burlutskaya Z., Zubkova D., Petryaeva A. Complex Modelling of Regional Tourism Systems // Sustainable development and engineering economics. 2023. Vol. 3. pp. 67–85.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Regional tourism depends on consumer incomes, the attributes of tourism services and the development of infrastructure. The paper examines which modeling tools can capture these relationships and how to represent differences in consumption between income groups.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed methods for forecasting demand and proposed combining simulation and econometric modeling with analysis of multiple data sources. System dynamics would represent the effects of public support on infrastructure, while agent-based modeling would capture visitors’ preferences. The paper also explores how a constant elasticity of substitution function could be included in a computable general equilibrium model.

Findings

Using synthetic data modeled on Austria, the authors implemented an illustrative calculation in R. They estimated potential revenue from domestic tourism and the number of trips for four income groups. The exercise considered income levels, housing and household expenditure, and the elasticity of tourism consumption.

Practical relevance

The study provides an early step toward an integrated model. The example shows how disposable income and differences in consumption can be related to potential demand for different categories of vacation.
