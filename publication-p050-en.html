Digital Modeling of Sociotechnical and Socioeconomic Systems

Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Федяевская Д.Э., Поспелов К.Н., Ракова В.В.

Bibliographic citation

DOILibrary record

Research problem

The monograph covers digital modeling of systems shaped by technical processes, decisions by participants and a changing environment. It connects theoretical questions about the purpose and boundaries of a model with the steps involved in building it and using it.

Approach and methods

The book examines conceptual modeling, the definition of objectives, major digital modeling paradigms and their use in hybrid models. Its methodological chapters cover formalization, data collection and preprocessing, implementation, verification, validation and calibration. Separate sections examine model adequacy and robustness, experiment planning and interpretation of results. The authors also consider the role of digital twins and ways to bring technical and social components together within a model.

Findings

Three examples show how the methods can be used to assess the consumer value of a tourism product, optimize a mechanical engineering production program and model social welfare provision. They illustrate how the representation of a process and the use of model output depend on the management question.

Practical relevance

The book is intended for researchers and students, including doctoral candidates. It can be consulted when designing a model, selecting an appropriate level of detail and planning computational experiments to support management decisions.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
