Designing an Intellectual Property Management System for a High-Tech Enterprise

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Лундаева К.А., Гинцяк А.М. Проектирование системы управления интеллектуальной собственностью наукоемкого предприятия // Управленческие науки. 2026. Т. 16. № 2. С. 154–166.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Intellectual property management in a high-tech enterprise spans research, legal protection, implementation, and commercialization. If these functions are organizationally separate, decisions made at one stage may be overlooked at later stages. The authors design a system that coordinates the management of intellectual assets with the planning and execution of research.

Approach and methods

The study uses a systems approach and structural-functional modeling. It specifies functional blocks, their inputs and outputs, and the feedback between them. Management tasks are mapped to stages in the life cycle of research outputs. The model accounts for technical conditions, legal protection, economic criteria, and technology maturity.

Findings

The results include a structural-functional model of intellectual property management and a scheme for integrating component models into the enterprise's wider research management system. The framework shows how information on legal protection and commercialization can inform revisions of strategic and tactical decisions.

Practical relevance

The structure provides a basis for developing connected algorithms and information tools. It helps establish which information departments should exchange between stages, which indicators to track, and when potential applications and uses of research results should be evaluated.

Research areas
