Research problem

The volume collects papers presented at the second Russian student research conference on innovation management and digital transformation, held in St. Petersburg on April 7–8, 2023. The imprint identifies Aleksei Gintciak as the volume’s responsible editor.

Approach and methods

The proceedings are organized into five sections covering innovation management, decision-making, projects, product development and promotion, and quality management. Individual contributions draw on systems analysis, business analysis and mathematical tools alongside methods specific to their application areas. Several papers deal with project management and the application of quality management tools in enterprises.

Findings

Topics include regional innovation systems and the game-theoretic study of stakeholder interactions. Individual contributions also study how information technology and data analysis can be used for product development and production processes. The Russian-language papers are accompanied by English-language bibliographic information and abstracts.

Practical relevance

The collection shows how students and early-career researchers formulate management problems in organizational systems. Its contributions offer examples for seminars and starting points for further studies in these subject areas.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
