Control of Innovation Processes

Туккель И.Л., Гинцяк А.М., Сурина А.В.

Bibliographic citation

Library record

Research problem

This textbook draws on a two-semester master's course at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University on the foundations of innovation and the management of innovation processes. It addresses analysis, design and development at the levels of organizations, regions and wider innovation systems.

Approach and methods

The first part covers the history of innovation theory, systems analysis, national and regional innovation systems, production functions, models of innovation development and the management of multiple projects and products. The second part examines innovation ecosystems, diffusion, technology readiness, and innovation infrastructure. Separate chapters introduce technology consulting and the design of alternative innovation system configurations.

Findings

The textbook connects models of innovation development to decisions about defining problems, setting objectives and criteria, designing development programs and selecting mechanisms for implementation. Its review questions help students check their understanding of the approaches covered in the chapters.

Practical relevance

The textbook is intended for master's students in Innovation Studies. Individual chapters can support course projects on designing an innovation ecosystem or comparing alternative models and management mechanisms for a particular development program.
