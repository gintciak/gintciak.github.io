Modeling of Social Processes Welfare Using a Discrete-Event Approach

Гинцяк А., Бурлуцкая Ж., Федяевская Д., Сидорина Е.

Research problem

The paper sets out a modeling task for the provision of housing to orphans and people who grew up without parental care. Comparing funding options requires tracking entry into the waiting list, the availability of resources and the allocation of housing. The modeling approach needs to fit the available records and produce measures that can be compared across scenarios.

Approach and methods

The authors reviewed agent-based simulation, system dynamics and discrete-event simulation for social policy applications. Individual behavioral rules would have required additional observations. Aggregate system dynamics also offered limited scope to represent differences in queue priorities and waiting times.

Findings

The authors selected discrete-event simulation. In the proposed model, events run from a person's addition to the waiting list through to the allocation of housing. The framework can accommodate different priorities, delays and changes in the funding available during the year. Planned outputs include the size of the queue, the number of people leaving it and average waiting time.

Practical relevance

The paper provides requirements for subsequent model development with the Novosibirsk municipal social policy department. They support the design of experiments to estimate how funding choices could affect the queue and the time needed to provide housing.
