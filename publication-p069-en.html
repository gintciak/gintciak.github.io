Problems and Challenges of Digitalization in Third-Party Logistics Companies

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Карванен О.А., Гинцяк А.М. Проблемы и вызовы цифровизации в компаниях 3PL-логистики // Интеллектуальная инженерная экономика и Индустрия 5.0 (ЭКОПРОМ): Сборник трудов международной научно-практической конференции. 2024. С. 567–573.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

Warehouse digitalization at a third-party logistics provider involves several organizations. A shared technical solution has to accommodate differences in equipment, software and customer workflows. This paper examines obstacles that logistics providers face when selecting and implementing such solutions in the Russian market.

Approach and methods

The authors review digital technologies in use, the experience of providers working with customers and the functional limits of warehouse management systems. They discuss examples requiring changes on both sides, including appointment slots for unloading, printing barcodes on transport packaging and using a client's own warehouse management software.

Findings

The analysis groups the difficulties into database integration, rapid equipment obsolescence, limited warehouse management functions and differences in technological maturity across participants. The examples show how a change that saves warehouse labour may require new procedures for a customer. The distribution of costs and benefits helps explain why providers and customers can assign different priorities to the same proposal.

Practical relevance

The authors propose assessing a solution before implementation and agreeing on contract terms that reflect each participant's capabilities. The categories can help providers identify issues for discussion with customers and decide what information to collect when evaluating a warehouse automation project.

Research areas

  • Transport modelling and optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems
