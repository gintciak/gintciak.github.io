A guide for mentors of student projects in the Fundamentals of Project Work course

Research problem

The guide addresses university instructors who mentor student teams in SPbPU's Fundamentals of Project Work course. Over a semester, teams choose a problem, develop a project solution and present a resulting product. The mentor guides second-year students with different backgrounds through this process and helps the team work with the project supervisor and the client.

Approach and methods

The authors connect the mentor's teaching responsibilities to the stages of project work. They describe the course organization, participant roles, online assignments and assessment of the theoretical and practical components. They also explain how to collect project ideas, form teams and organize work on problems proposed by external clients.

Findings

The guide provides scenarios for starting projects, holding progress meetings and running final presentations. It discusses ways to give feedback, review individual contributions and prepare teams for the university project competition. Appendices contain assessment criteria, sample checklists and team-building exercises.

Practical relevance

Instructors can use the material to plan the semester, prepare mentoring sessions and discuss expected outcomes with teams. The scenarios can be adapted to clarify what the project product should achieve and to identify stages at which a team needs additional help.
