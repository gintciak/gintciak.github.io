Digital Modelling of Production Processes

Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н.

Bibliographic citation

DOILibrary record

Research problem

This teaching guide treats digital modeling as a way to support managerial decisions in production. It connects the analysis of an operational problem with the description of the system, model development and computational experimentation.

Approach and methods

Four chapters progress through conceptual, mathematical and simulation models, from data preparation and model design to practical work. The book covers process description in Business Process Model and Notation, missing data and outliers, distribution fitting, verification and validation. AnyLogic exercises cover process flows, resources, events, schedules, batches, queues, statistics and simulation experiments.

Findings

The final chapter offers eight case formulations. These include a transport network, warehouse layout, aircraft component assembly, a chemical and metallurgical process, and an oil field. The cases illustrate how the choice of data and representation changes with the application.

Practical relevance

The guide is designed for classroom use and independent work by students and graduate researchers. Readers can use the exercises to develop a model and justify an experiment, then select a sectoral case as the basis for a course project.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Intelligent decision support systems
