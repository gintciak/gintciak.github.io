Conducting Research as a Master's Student in Quality Management or Innovation Studies

Редько С.Г., Гинцяк А.М., Селедцова И.А., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Поспелов К.Н., Бекетов С.М.

Research problem

This guide addresses master's students in Quality Management and Innovation Studies. It explains how to turn an initial interest into a research question, organize work across several semesters and communicate results in a master's thesis, conference presentation or article.

Approach and methods

The authors show how the object and subject of study, practical and scientific problems, research objectives, methods and results relate to one another. They discuss selecting a topic, planning work by semester and working with a supervisor. Other chapters cover bibliographic and full-text databases, developing search queries, screening sources and preparing materials for journals and conferences.

Findings

The guide brings together requirements for the structure, content and presentation of a master's thesis, along with assessment criteria and practical advice. Examples illustrate how to ground claims of novelty in the research findings and explain their practical significance.

Practical relevance

The material can be used in research seminars or for individual study planning. Students and supervisors can use a common sequence of stages to discuss the literature search, refine the research problem and assess whether a text is ready for defence or presentation to a research audience.

Research areas

  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems
