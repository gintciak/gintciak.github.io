Assessment of Digital Maturity of Enterprises

D.A. Levaniuk, M V Bolsunovskaya, S V Shirokova, A M Gintciak

Bibliographic citation

D.A. Levaniuk, M V Bolsunovskaya, S V Shirokova, A M Gintciak. Assessment of Digital Maturity of Enterprises // IOP Conference Series: Materials Science and Engineering. IOP Publishing. 2020. Vol. 986. No. 1. Article 012019.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

The published text examines how project management methodology can be adapted to oil and gas IT projects. It asks how general project management guidance can be applied within a particular enterprise while respecting its operating conditions and the requirements of the information system being built.

Approach and methods

The authors review existing methodologies and describe a corporate standard covering project roles, life-cycle stages and checkpoints. Their example is a project to automate the monitoring of key performance indicators. The case shows how the standard connects the roadmap and budget with the readiness of specific project outputs.

Findings

The article presents a life-cycle structure, examples of checkpoints and recommendations for applying the corporate standard to IT projects. Checkpoints provide information about the current state of the work and connect schedule oversight with progress on the product.

Practical relevance

The results can inform the adaptation of project procedures and the selection of verifiable deliverables for each stage. The title follows the publisher's metadata. This description follows the abstract and the body of the paper, which discuss project methods.
