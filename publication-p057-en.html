Development of an Interactive Online Course on Game Theory in the Innovation Processes Management

Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М. Разработка интерактивного онлайн-курса по теории игр в управлении инновационными процессами // Преподавание информационных технологий в Российской Федерации: Сборник научных трудов Двадцать первой открытой Всероссийской конференции. 2023. С. 197–199.

Publication pageFull text in the proceedings

Research problem

The paper presents the design of a course in which master’s students use game theory to analyze decisions in organizational systems. The teaching goal is to connect mathematical concepts with innovation management situations where the outcome depends on the actions of several participants.

Approach and methods

The introductory component combines videos and exercises on game classification, dominant strategies, Nash equilibrium, extensive form and auctions. The applied component covers product development, schedules, procurement, teams and risks. Some of the activities ask several learners to choose strategies simultaneously and then interpret what happened.

Findings

The publication sets out the course structure and describes the stages of developing the materials. Applied topics combine a lecture, exercises of different difficulty and interactive scenarios. The scenarios put learners in the same game and provide an opportunity to discuss the result of their combined decisions. A pilot offering is part of the proposed development process.

Practical relevance

The course was designed for master’s students in innovation studies and as an elective for students in other programs. Discussing the combined decisions allows learners to see how predictions depend on assumptions about the other players.

Research areas
