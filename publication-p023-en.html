High-level Simulation Model of Tourism Industry Dynamics

Aleksei М. Gintciak, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Alexandra А. Petryaeva

Bibliographic citation

Aleksei М. Gintciak, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Alexandra А. Petryaeva. Укрупненная имитационная модель динамики туристической отрасли // Business Informatics. 2022. Т. 16. № 3. С. 53–67.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

This article examines the relationship between the state of the economy and domestic tourism. It seeks to represent changes in visitor flows, revenue per tourist and the contribution of tourism to gross domestic product. The motivating context is recovery of the sector after the pandemic.

Approach and methods

The authors build a system dynamics model using data from open sources. They describe the functional relationships among indicators and use scenario experiments to check the logic of those relationships. The paper also reviews computable general equilibrium models as tools for studying supply and demand for tourism products.

Findings

The study produces a high-level model of domestic tourism. Its experiments assess how the model connects visitor flows, revenue from tourism products and the contribution of tourism to the economy. The authors position the model as a first stage that can be extended to include constraints on labour and natural resources and differences among groups of consumers.

Practical relevance

The model offers a basis for discussing recovery scenarios. It helps trace the relationships through which changes in demand and revenue affect industry indicators and the economy.
