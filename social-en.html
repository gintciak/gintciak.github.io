Research

Modelling socioeconomic systems

Regional development, tourism and the provision of public services.

I study systems in which changes at the level of individuals or organizations affect sectoral or regional outcomes. The choice among system dynamics, discrete-event simulation and agent-based modelling depends on which relationships need to be represented.

With colleagues, I have developed studies on regional tourism, innovation development and public services. They examine ways to bring together different statistical sources and process descriptions to compare scenarios and understand where the conclusions apply.
