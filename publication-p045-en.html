The Model of Rational Stimulation of Project Team Members Based on Game Theory Tools

Никитина М.И., Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Зубкова Д.А.

Bibliographic citation

Никитина М.И., Гинцяк А.М., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Зубкова Д.А. Модель рационального стимулирования членов проектной команды на базе инструментов теории игр // Прикладная математика и вопросы управления. 2023. № 2. С. 72–88.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

During the conflict stage of team formation, a project manager needs to encourage participation in a shared task and justify the cost of incentives. The article formalizes the choice of payments using the properties of the task and the relationships within the team.

Approach and methods

The authors adapt a hierarchical model of collective incentives with a management center and team members. The model includes task difficulty, priority expressed as the number of workers needed, and the connections among workers. It simplifies the calculations by dispensing with an initial classification of workers into types. The authors illustrate its use by calculating payments for a range of combinations of priority and difficulty.

Findings

Under the study conditions, the cost to the management center was more sensitive to the number of workers required than to the difficulty of the task. The authors also show how priority and difficulty can indicate when an additional incentive is needed. They identify individual competencies as an aspect to incorporate in future versions.

Practical relevance

The approach gives a manager a basis for estimating incentive payments for a given task. The result can support a discussion of the available budget and the team participation needed to achieve the desired priority.

Research areas
