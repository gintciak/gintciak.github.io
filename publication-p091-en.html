A Hierarchical Project Portfolio Management Method Based on Pareto-Optimal Labor Allocation Algorithms

Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Бекетов С.М., Гинцяк А.М. Метод иерархического управления портфелем проектов на основе алгоритмов Парето-оптимального распределения трудовых ресурсов // Известия Самарского научного центра Российской академии наук. 2025. Т. 27. № 3 (125). С. 179–188.

Publication pageFull text

Research problem

Staffing decisions for individual projects affect whether a portfolio of concurrent projects can be delivered with the available workforce. Allocation should account for the availability of each skill group, differences in duration and labor requirements, and uncertainty in those estimates. The paper addresses how team selection and subsequent workload balancing can be connected in a single planning procedure.

Approach and methods

The method uses two levels of optimization. A project network, estimated work content, and feasible team configurations are prepared for each project. Expected duration and labor requirements are used to construct a Pareto front, whose convergence is then assessed. Project-level alternatives are combined subject to portfolio resource constraints. Once a portfolio scenario has been selected, the method reallocates staff dynamically to smooth workload.

Findings

The authors demonstrate the sequence on four mobile application development projects. They construct project-specific fronts and a portfolio front that accounts for variability in the estimates. A selected scenario illustrates how the utilization profiles change after balancing.

Practical relevance

The procedure gives portfolio managers a choice among scenarios with different durations, labor requirements, and staffing profiles. It is intended for projects organized around sequential stages and requires data on task dependencies, workforce availability, and variability in task effort.
