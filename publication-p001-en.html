The Substantiation of the Project Management System for Company by Means of the Functional Completeness Criteria in Innovative Company

Svetlana V. Shirokova, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Alexey M. Gintciak, Mriya S. Zharova

Bibliographic citation

Svetlana V. Shirokova, Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Alexey M. Gintciak, Mriya S. Zharova. The Substantiation of the Project Management System for Company by Means of the Functional Completeness Criteria in Innovative Company // Proceedings of the 3rd International Conference Ergo-2018: Human Factors in Complex Technical Systems and Environments, Ergo 2018. IEEE. 2018. pp. 40–44.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

An IT services company needs a project management system that supports task planning, progress monitoring and staff workload management. The paper addresses this choice for a company that implements and maintains information systems and needs to understand whether it can accept additional projects with the people it already has.

Approach and methods

The authors compare six products, including Bitrix24, Megaplan, Jira and Easy Redmine. They express requirements as a set of functions, then use this representation to calculate pairwise measures of functionality and similarity, including a Jaccard measure. The final selection also takes into account the prices of different deployment options and the company's specific requirements.

Findings

Bitrix24, Megaplan and Easy Redmine cover the functional requirements most comprehensively. The authors recommend Easy Redmine for the case examined. The cost of the on-premises edition and an explicit requirement for project issue tracking informed this choice.

Practical relevance

The paper also proposes a personnel management module. A calendar view would show overloaded staff, support changes to task dates and assignments, and help managers assess capacity for new projects. The comparison can be adapted to other software selection problems by revisiting the requirements and candidate products.
