Complex Method of the Consumer Value Estimation on the Way to Risk-Free and Sustainable Production

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Daria A. Zubkova, Alexandra A. Petryaeva, Darya E. Fedyaevskaya

Bibliographic citation

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Daria A. Zubkova, Alexandra A. Petryaeva, Darya E. Fedyaevskaya. Complex Method of the Consumer Value Estimation on the Way to Risk-Free and Sustainable Production // Sustainability. 2023. Vol. 15. No. 2. Article 1273.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Product design requires an understanding of which attributes matter to different customer groups. Existing assessment practices produce heterogeneous measures that are difficult to compare. The authors develop a common procedure that can be specified for a particular product, market and set of input data.

Approach and methods

The paper reviews consumer value assessment methods and compares tools for constructing aggregate indicators. The proposed procedure begins by segmenting the market and arranging attributes valued by customers in a hierarchy. Scales and weights are chosen for the indicators. Values are combined within each group and then aggregated to obtain an overall assessment for the segment.

Findings

The study produces a sequence of assessment steps and a general aggregation formula. An example illustrates the procedure using expert assessments of tourism product attributes. The authors identify field testing on real data for products in multiple industries as a subsequent step.

Practical relevance

The procedure can support decisions about which attributes to develop or improve in a product or service. Making customer priorities explicit helps justify how design resources are allocated. The results depend on the segments, evaluation scales and data used to implement the method.
