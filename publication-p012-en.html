Comparative Analysis of Approaches to the Specialists’ Competences Development in Innovative Industries

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Inna V. Yudina, Pavel S. Kozlovskiy

Bibliographic citation

Marina V. Bolsunovskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Inna V. Yudina, Pavel S. Kozlovskiy. Comparative Analysis of Approaches to the Specialists’ Competences Development in Innovative Industries // Proceedings of the 2nd International Conference on Education Science and Social Development (ESSD 2019). Atlantis Press. 2019.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

Rapid changes in technology affect the skills required of specialists in high-technology industries. This study asks how universities and employers can divide responsibilities for developing those skills. It also considers how an understanding of individual students' characteristics can inform educational and career decisions.

Approach and methods

The authors compare academic education with learning through professional activity. They organise the comparison around changes in learning outcomes, the content of skills and the types of competency each setting can develop. They discuss tracking student development over time and recording results in an electronic portfolio.

Findings

The authors argue that universities should focus on cultural, general professional and foundational competencies, while advanced specialisation should largely occur in the workplace. Cooperation between university career guidance teams and company human resources departments is proposed as a way to connect the two stages.

Practical relevance

The analysis is relevant to programmes that involve workplace experience and formal partnerships with employers. An electronic portfolio could bring together information that helps students and their advisers choose subsequent training opportunities.
