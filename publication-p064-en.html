Data Preprocessing for Modeling Socioeconomic Systems in View of Uncertainty

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Tatiana Kudryavtseva

Bibliographic citation

Kapiton N. Pospelov, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Tatiana Kudryavtseva. Data Preprocessing for Modeling Socioeconomic Systems in View of Uncertainty // Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems: Understanding the Digital Transformation of Socio-Economic-Technological Systems. Springer Nature Switzerland. 2024. Vol. 951. pp. 151–161.

Publication pageDOI

Research problem

The chapter addresses how to prepare model inputs when the parameters of a socioeconomic system are uncertain. Evidence about individual behaviour, economic indicators and other events may vary in type and completeness. The modelling team has to decide how to represent this uncertainty before setting up the main computational study.

Approach and methods

The authors compare maximum likelihood estimation, Bayesian data analysis, spline interpolation, probability distribution approximation and an imprecise probability model based on hierarchical intervals. They consider the conditions under which each method is applicable and discuss the speed and accuracy of preprocessing. Monte Carlo methods are considered for working with probabilities after a representation of the uncertain parameters has been chosen.

Findings

The chapter proposes a decision tree that uses the features of the modelling problem and expert judgement to guide the choice of a preprocessing method. The framework organizes methods that require different prior information and types of observations. Its empirical basis is limited. Formal decision criteria and testing of the tree across additional socioeconomic systems are identified as future work.

Practical relevance

The framework can be used during model design to document assumptions about uncertain inputs and to select a preprocessing procedure. This makes it easier to explain how the probabilistic descriptions were obtained and what they mean for the interpretation of scenarios.

Research areas

  • Simulation-based optimization
  • Modelling socioeconomic systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems

data preprocessing, uncertainty, socioeconomic systems, Bayesian analysis, simulation modelling, decision tree
