Development of a Configurable Course on Digital Modeling of Production Processes

Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М., Бекетов С.М., Поспелов К.Н. Разработка конфигурируемого курса по цифровому моделированию производственных процессов // Информатизация образования и науки. 2026. № 3 (71). С. 6–22.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

A course in digital production modeling must retain a coherent method as it is adapted to learners with different backgrounds. The authors set out to design a family of related courses for undergraduate, master's and continuing professional education.

Approach and methods

The course is designed as an educational system with a shared methodological core and optional modules. The core covers problem formulation, formalization of the system, model building, computational experiments and interpretation. Module choices depend on the target competencies, the required depth of coverage and available teaching time. Configuration parameters also address the learning format, the degree of student autonomy and assessment methods.

Findings

The paper describes a core master's course, an introductory undergraduate version, an extended version for a specialist master's degree, and professional development variants for educators and industry staff. The configurations range from 72 to 360 hours. All follow the same modeling sequence while adjusting practical work and the choice of tools to the audience.

Practical relevance

The architecture gives instructors a basis for assembling coherent variants of a course. Putting it into practice calls for consistent module descriptions, explicit prerequisites, coordinated assessment and the ability to select an appropriate configuration for a particular program.
