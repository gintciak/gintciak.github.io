AI-Supported Decision Making in Multi-Agent Production Systems Using the Example of the Oil and Gas Industry

Polina A. Sharko, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Daria A. Zubkova, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Kapiton N. Pospelov

Bibliographic citation

Polina A. Sharko, Zhanna V. Burlutskaya, Daria A. Zubkova, Aleksei M. Gintciak, Kapiton N. Pospelov. AI-Supported Decision Making in Multi-Agent Production Systems Using the Example of the Oil and Gas Industry // Applied Sciences. 2025. Vol. 15. No. 10. Article 5366.

Publication pageDOIFull text

Research problem

In a distributed production system, units control different resources and pursue their own performance targets. The paper asks how to formalize their interactions when they manage shared resources. It examines how to connect organizational structure, local objectives and the actions available to individual participants.

Approach and methods

The authors develop ontologies for agent interactions and for management of the production system. They describe agent behaviour using the belief-desire-intention (BDI) architecture. An oil and gas case specifies management levels, production clusters and the drilling and well-intervention crews allocated to them. Game theory is used to express agent utility for actions that include doing work independently and sharing resources.

Findings

The case shows how a general ontology can be instantiated for resource allocation between clusters. The formalization links resources and oil production objectives to the choices available to an agent. The case study demonstrates the structure of the proposed models using information from an oil and gas enterprise.

Practical relevance

The models can inform the design of decision support for distributed choices about transferring crews between sites. An application in another industry would require identifying the local objectives, possible actions and observable characteristics of its production agents.

Research areas

  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Game theory and strategic interaction
