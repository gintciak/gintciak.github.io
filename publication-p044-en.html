Modeling R&D Projects Using Game Theory Tools

Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М. Моделирование научно-исследовательских проектов с применением инструментов теории игр // Инновации. 2023. № 1 (291). С. 66–73.

Publication pageFull text

Research problem

In an R&D project, the funding organization, project leader and team members can have different interests. A stable combination of their decisions can still produce an outcome well below the maximum collective payoff. The article proposes a common framework for describing and analyzing such situations.

Approach and methods

The framework uses an ontology to connect project participants, interests and strategic interactions with the elements of a game. The procedure for deriving a case-specific model includes selecting the interaction, defining the players, strategies and utilities, classifying the game and choosing a suitable analytical approach.

Findings

The work provides a general model and a procedure for deriving specific games from it. For an individual game, the author proposes finding equilibrium profiles, examining their sensitivity to model parameters, and comparing them with a profile that maximizes collective utility. The gap allows management to formulate changes to the interaction and examine their likely consequences.

Practical relevance

The model gives project managers a shared vocabulary for describing individual decisions. It can help them examine why an expected outcome falls short of a preferred one and develop reasons for changing how project stakeholders interact.

Research areas

  • Game theory and strategic interaction
  • Project and portfolio management
  • Intelligent decision support systems

Keywords

game theory, R&D projects, stakeholders, equilibrium, ontology modeling
