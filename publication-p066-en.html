Intelligent Decision-Making Support System Architecture for Oil Production Enterprises

Бекетов С.М., Федяевская Д.Э., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Гинцяк А.М.

Bibliographic citation

Бекетов С.М., Федяевская Д.Э., Бурлуцкая Ж.В., Гинцяк А.М. Архитектура интеллектуальной системы поддержки принятия решений для нефтедобывающих предприятий // Автоматизация и информатизация ТЭК. 2024. № 11 (616). С. 16–26.

eLIBRARY record

Research problem

The paper designs a decision support system for oil production enterprises where data and analytical functions are distributed across departments. It considers how to integrate these functions and move from existing business processes to a target architecture in which field development scenarios can be assessed against common criteria.

Approach and methods

The authors use ArchiMate to describe the existing systems and processes, the target architecture and the information exchanged by its components. They propose a common data repository, a service bus and a control module for message routing and transformation. A scenario optimizer receives calculations for options involving existing wells and the commissioning of new wells. It uses production and cost to identify nondominated alternatives.

Findings

The study presents a conceptual architecture for a shared information environment and identifies the processes and data flows that would need to change during implementation. In the design, analytical modules supply predictions and other calculations, the control module coordinates the exchange, and the optimizer presents trade-offs between production and cost to decision makers.

Practical relevance

The design can inform requirements for integrating existing systems, standardizing inputs and developing a scenario optimization module. Applying it to another enterprise would involve specifying its business processes, data sources and scenario selection criteria.

Research areas

  • Intelligent decision support systems
  • Digital modelling of production systems
  • Multi-objective and stochastic optimization

Keywords

decision support, enterprise architecture, oil production, ArchiMate, business process reengineering, scenario optimization
