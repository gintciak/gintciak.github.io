Organizational Systems as Control Object

Гинцяк А.М., Поспелов К.Н.

Bibliographic citation

Гинцяк А.М., Поспелов К.Н. Организационные системы как объект управления. Санкт-Петербург: ПОЛИТЕХ-ПРЕСС. 2025. 52 с. ISBN 978-5-7422-9113-8.

DOILibrary record

Research problem

This textbook introduces methods from engineering and cybernetics for the study of organizational systems. Its focus is on organizations in which decisions depend on structural relationships, resource availability, and participant behavior. The intended audience is doctoral students in the specialty of Control in Organizational Systems.

Approach and methods

The material starts with the development of organizational systems theory and its basic concepts. It then describes modeling tasks and develops a rationale for treating complex organizational systems as a distinct subject. Relevant characteristics include heterogeneous components and relationships, openness to the environment, changing tasks, and adaptation of organizational structure. A dedicated section examines how to represent decision makers whose access to information and capacity to process it are limited.

Findings

The treatment of bounded rationality relates characteristics of a management problem to options for representing behavior. It identifies situations in which these assumptions can affect the design and interpretation of a model. Together, the chapters establish a vocabulary for relating the organizational structure, the control mechanism, and the properties of individual participants.

Practical relevance

The textbook supports preparation for the doctoral qualifying examination. It also helps researchers define their system boundaries, describe relevant participants, and justify behavioral assumptions when formulating a study.

Research areas

  • Multi-agent systems
