DOILibrary record

Research problem

The volume collects papers presented at the fourth national student research conference on Innovation Management in the Context of Digital Transformation, held on 11 and 12 April 2025 at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University. Aleksei Gintciak served as executive editor.

Approach and methods

Its contributions cover organizational systems, mathematical methods in innovation management, project management, industrial digitalization, and quality management. The proceedings include research by students and doctoral candidates, as well as staff from universities, research organizations, and industry.

Practical relevance

The collection offers examples of systems analysis, modeling, and optimization applied to organizational and industrial problems. It was published by POLYTECH-PRESS and comprises 360 pages, ISBN 978-5-7422-9134-3.
